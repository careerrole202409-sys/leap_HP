About Us

At Leap, just as we develop our products from scratch, we build our organizational culture and the company itself from the ground up. We aim for a society where every company can take a leap onto the global stage.

Mission

Empowering every company to take a leap globally

Leap's mission is to "empower every company to take a leap globally." Our purpose is not to support superficial growth driven solely by numbers, but to facilitate true "leaps"—crossing borders and delivering essential value in new markets.

Our Culture

We value three core principles to deliver the best results to our clients with maximum speed.

Responsibility

Each of us takes full responsibility for the quality of our output for our clients, executing every task as a true professional.

Focus

To maximize our output, we concentrate our resources solely on what matters most.

Speed

By staying focused, we act faster than anyone else, delivering results at a speed that outpaces market changes.

History

Leap was founded in July 2024 by two cousins, Hara and Tsuruoka, who had aimed to contribute to society through digital transformation (DX) since their days at the University of Tokyo.

From his experience with macroeconomic policy at the Cabinet Office, Hara was convinced that "the global leap of Japanese companies is the key to revitalizing the Japanese economy." Meanwhile, Tsuruoka, who was engaged in generative AI research and development at Hitachi, realized that AI advancements were dramatically lowering the barriers to global expansion, such as language and business customs.

"Within the next 10 years, it will become standard for companies worldwide to operate globally."

With this conviction, the two established Leap in Yokohama to support corporate challenges through technology and strategy.
