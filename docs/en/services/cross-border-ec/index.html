Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Which countries and marketplaces are supported?
Currently, we support Shopee for Southeast Asia/Taiwan, Lazada for Southeast Asia, Coupang for South Korea, Amazon Global Selling for the US/Europe/Japan, and Rakuten for Japan.
How long does it take to start listing?
Mall account opening and listing are usually completed in 2 weeks to 1 month after you enter the necessary information. It may be faster if you already have the information ready.
Is it okay if I don't speak English or the local language?
Yes, that is not a problem. Leap provides features to support language barriers, such as translation of product information and templated customer support.
Can you tell me more about the pricing structure?
Cross-border EC features are available on the Standard plan or higher. A monthly fee of 20,000 JPY (excluding tax) is charged for each marketplace you wish to list on or integrate with.
