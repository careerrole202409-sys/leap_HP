Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What languages are supported?
We support a total of 11 languages: English, Chinese (Simplified/Traditional), German, French, Spanish, Korean, Vietnamese, Thai, Malay, and Indonesian.
Who owns the copyright of created videos?
The commercial usage rights for content generated by Leap belong to the customer. You can freely use them on your own website, SNS ads, etc.
Are the video voices natural?
Yes. We use the latest speech synthesis technology to generate natural speech that is close to native speakers with emotion, rather than just reading text.
How is the pricing calculated?
You are charged based on the number of languages you wish to use. For example, if you want to make videos in English and Chinese, the fee will be for 2 languages (40,000 JPY/month). The free plan allows you to try image generation only.
