Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Which SNS platforms are supported?
Currently, we support the 4 major platforms: Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, and X (formerly Twitter).
How accurate is the auto-translation?
Leap's AI goes beyond literal translation; it handles casual expressions unique to SNS and localizes hashtags, creating posts that are naturally accepted by local users.
Can I connect existing overseas accounts?
Yes, you can. Simply connect your existing accounts and configure the language settings to start auto-translated posting immediately.
Can you tell me more about the pricing structure?
Manual posting and connection features are available on the Free plan. If you use auto-translated posting, a monthly fee of 20,000 JPY (excluding tax) applies per target language.
