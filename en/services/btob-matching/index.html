Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Which countries have the most distributors?
Currently, many distributors from Singapore, Hong Kong, the USA, China, and Taiwan are registered. We are gradually expanding areas, focusing on Southeast Asia and the West.
Are there commissions if a deal is closed?
No, Leap is a matching platform and charges no commissions (margins) on transactions. You only pay the monthly subscription fee.
Can I use messaging if I don't speak English?
Yes. The platform's messaging function has built-in translation. Input in your native language, and it is automatically translated and sent in the recipient's language.
