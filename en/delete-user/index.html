How to Delete Your Account

Established October 28, 2025

1

Log in to Leap

Please log in to Leap using the URL below.

https://app.leap.site/top
2

Go to My Page

Select "My Page" from the header to navigate to your profile.

3

Open Settings

Click the "Account Settings" button in the top right corner.

4

Click Delete Account Button

Click the "Delete Account" button.

5

Confirm Deletion

A message will appear asking "Your information will be completely deleted from Leap. Are you sure?" Type "delete" and click the OK button. Your account information will be permanently deleted from Leap after one week.

⚠️ Important Notice

Once one week has passed after account deletion, all data will be permanently deleted and cannot be recovered. Please back up any necessary data before deletion.
