## Quick Overview: Selling on Etsy from Japan Comes Down to Payoneer and Four Fees

To sell internationally on Etsy from Japan, you register with Payoneer (a payment service that lets you receive overseas sales), open an Etsy shop, and list your items. Sellers based in Japan can sign up for Etsy Payments (Etsy's payment system) using a Payoneer account.

You can understand the costs by focusing on four fees: 1. the listing fee (0.20 USD per item), 2. the transaction fee (6.5% of the order total), 3. the payment processing fee (6.0% + 0.30 USD for bank accounts in Japan), and 4. the Offsite Ads fee (12-15%, only on orders from those ads). This article walks through the setup steps and fees as confirmed in Etsy's official Help Center, works through the fees on a 52 USD order, and explains how to combine Etsy with your own brand store.

## What Is Etsy?

Etsy is a US-based online marketplace (a platform where many independent shops sell side by side) for buying and selling handmade items, vintage goods, and craft supplies.

### Reach Buyers Around the World

Etsy's biggest strength is that your work is shown to a large global audience that is already there. According to Etsy's Q3 2026 shareholder letter, the Etsy marketplace had approximately 88 million active buyers (people who purchased in the past 12 months) as of September 30, 2026. Listings are automatically translated into each shopper's language, so you can even start by listing in Japanese.

### What You Can Sell on Etsy

You can only sell items that meet Etsy's Creativity Standards. The Help Center lists four categories:

1. **Made by a seller**: items you make yourself, by hand or with tools such as 3D printers
2. **Designed by a seller**: your own designs produced by a production partner, and digital items
3. **Handpicked by a seller**: vintage items at least 20 years old and curated collections
4. **Sourced by a seller**: items that enable buyer creativity, such as craft supplies and personalized items

Reselling mass-produced items is not allowed in the handmade category. Prohibited items such as alcohol, tobacco, drugs, and weapons cannot be listed either, so check the Help Center before you list.

## How to Open an Etsy Shop from Japan

The smoothest order is: register with Payoneer, open your shop, list your items, then set up shipping. Menu names follow Etsy's official Help Center as of November 2026.

### 1. Register with Payoneer

Sellers in Japan can sign up for Etsy Payments with a Payoneer account, and the bank account is verified during Payoneer setup. Payoneer's fees are separate from Etsy's. According to Payoneer's FAQ, an Annual Account Fee of 29.95 USD may be charged starting 12 months after registration unless you receive more than 6,000 USD (or equivalent) in any consecutive 12-month period.

### 2. Open Your Etsy Shop

Shop setup must be done in a desktop web browser, in this order:

1. Create an Etsy account, then go to "Sell on Etsy" and choose "Open your Etsy shop"
2. Choose your shop language, country, and currency, then save
3. Pick a shop name (4-20 characters, no spaces or special characters)
4. Choose how you'll get paid and add billing information (sellers outside the US must add a credit or debit card in addition to bank details)
5. Complete identity verification (ID and a selfie) and turn on two-factor authentication
6. Choose "Open your shop"

Once you add billing information, a one-time, non-refundable shop setup fee applies. The cost varies by location, and Payoneer users are charged in USD.

### 3. List Your Items

To create a listing, go to Shop Manager, select Listings, then Add a listing, fill in the form, and choose Publish. You can add up to 20 photos, a title of up to 140 characters, and up to 13 tags.

Listings are translated automatically, but your own manual translations take priority. Writing your titles, descriptions, and tags in English yourself makes them clearer to overseas shoppers. You can add manual translations under Shop Manager > Settings > Languages and translations.

### 4. Set Shipping Prices and Processing Times

Go to your Shipping settings and, under Shipping profiles, select Create profile. Enter the origin country and postal code, the processing time, and the shipping service, then set rates for "Everywhere Else" or for individual countries. Free shipping can only be selected for domestic shipping, so international rates are set as a fixed price. Processing times are counted in business days (Monday to Friday).

For small items shipped from Japan, Japan Post's International Air Packet (renamed from International ePacket Light on June 1, 2026) is a practical option. It handles parcels up to 2 kg with tracking, and the rate to the US for up to 100 g is 1,200 yen (official page as of November 2026).

### 5. Check the Rules for the EU and the US

If you sell to the EU, you may need to comply with the EU General Product Safety Regulation (GPSR). This can involve adding product safety information to listings and naming a responsible person in the EU. If you can't do this yet, you can opt out of selling to the EEA (European Economic Area) and Northern Ireland under Shop Manager > Settings > Options.

Items shipped to the US may be subject to duties. Etsy's US Estimated Tariffs Calculator lets you check expected duties and taxes based on an item's price, country of origin, and HS code (a customs classification number).

## Etsy Fees for Selling Internationally

Etsy's core fees are the listing fee, the transaction fee, and the payment processing fee. Depending on your situation, a currency conversion fee and an Offsite Ads fee may also apply (Etsy Help Center as of November 2026).

| Fee | Amount / rate | When it applies |
|---|---|---|
| Shop setup fee | Varies by location (one-time, non-refundable) | When you add billing information |
| Listing fee | 0.20 USD per item (listings last 4 months) | When you list or renew |
| Transaction fee | 6.5% of the order total (including shipping and gift wrap) | When an item sells |
| Payment processing fee (bank in Japan) | 6.0% + 0.30 USD of the total sale | When an item sells |
| Currency conversion fee | 2.5% of the sale amount | When your listing currency differs from your payment account currency |
| Offsite Ads fee | 15% of the sale (12% if you've had 10,000 USD or more in sales in a 365-day period), capped at 100 USD per order | When a sale comes from an ad |

### Offsite Ads Work Differently Above 10,000 USD in Sales

With Offsite Ads, Etsy promotes your items on external partner sites and charges a fee on orders placed within 30 days of a shopper clicking the ad. Shops that have never reached 10,000 USD in sales in any consecutive 365-day period can opt out. Once a shop reaches that level, participation becomes mandatory and the rate drops to 12%.

### Worked Example: A 40 USD Item with 12 USD Shipping

Using Etsy's published rates, here are the fees on a 40 USD item sold overseas with 12 USD shipping (a 52 USD order total). Sales tax is not included.

| Item | Calculation | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Listing fee | 1 item | 0.20 USD |
| Transaction fee | 52 USD × 6.5% | 3.38 USD |
| Payment processing fee | 52 USD × 6.0% + 0.30 USD | 3.42 USD |
| Total | About 13.5% of the order total | 7.00 USD |
| (With currency conversion) | + 52 USD × 2.5% | 8.30 USD total (about 16.0%) |
| (Via Offsite Ads) | + 52 USD × 15% | 14.80 USD total (about 28.5%) |

The core fees alone take more than 10% of the order total, and close to 30% on an Offsite Ads order. Actual postage (1,200 yen for up to 100 g to the US by International Air Packet), packaging, and Payoneer fees come on top, so set your prices so that you still make a profit after fees and shipping.

## Etsy vs. Your Own Brand Store

The best approach is to use Etsy as a place to be discovered by overseas shoppers and your own store as a place to build your brand and connect with fans. Etsy already has buyers, but fees scale with your sales and your shop runs under Etsy's rules. With your own store you handle marketing yourself, but you can use customer data for email newsletters and repeat sales.

## FAQ

**Q. Can I open an Etsy shop from Japan?**

A. Yes, you can open an Etsy shop from Japan. Sellers in Japan sign up for Etsy Payments with a Payoneer account and receive their sales through it.

**Q. Can I sell on Etsy without speaking English?**

A. Yes, you can sell without strong English. Listings are automatically translated into each shopper's language, but manual translations take priority, so preparing English titles and tags makes your items easier to understand.

**Q. What can't I sell on Etsy?**

A. Reselling mass-produced items in the handmade category is not allowed, and prohibited items such as alcohol, tobacco, drugs, and weapons cannot be listed.

**Q. Can I choose not to sell to the EU?**

A. Yes. If GPSR compliance is difficult, you can opt out of selling to the EEA and Northern Ireland under Shop Manager > Settings > Options.

## Summary and Key Takeaway

You can start selling on Etsy from Japan by registering with Payoneer and then opening your shop, listing items, and setting up shipping in order. The core fees are a 6.5% transaction fee and a 6.0% + 0.30 USD processing fee, with another 15% on Offsite Ads orders, so price your work on the assumption that 10-30% of the order total goes to fees.

Etsy is a great place to be discovered, but building long-term relationships with buyers is easier with a brand site of your own. Using Etsy to learn which countries and pieces resonate, then deepening those relationships on your own store, is a sustainable way for small brands to grow overseas.
