## Quick Overview: Mercari Shops Lets You Sell Abroad by Shipping Within Japan

Cross-border sales on Mercari Shops work like this: Mercari, or a cross-border ecommerce company officially partnered with Mercari, buys the item on behalf of an overseas customer and handles export, customs, and international shipping. The seller simply ships the item to a designated address in Japan, exactly as with a domestic order.

The fees are the same as for domestic sales, and there is no separate charge for cross-border orders (as of November 2026). Mercari Shops charges a 10% sales fee on the tax-inclusive price and 200 yen per payout, with no setup or monthly fees.

The trade-off is that Mercari or the partner company handles all communication with overseas buyers, so sellers do not build a direct relationship with those customers. This article walks through the mechanics, fees, and setup based on official sources, and explains how this differs from selling overseas through your own online store.

## How Cross-Border Sales Work on Mercari Shops

On Mercari Shops, the seller never deals with the overseas buyer directly. Instead, an intermediary buys the item as if it were a domestic customer. According to the official Mercari Shops website, communication with buyers, shipping, export, and customs are handled by a cross-border ecommerce partner or by Mercari, so sellers do not need to do anything special.

### The Order Flow

Official Mercari Shops articles and the Mercari Help Center describe the flow as follows:

1. An overseas buyer orders the item through a partner company's website or a Mercari app
2. The partner company, or an official Mercari account, purchases the item from the seller
3. The seller ships the item, in normal packaging, to a designated address in Japan
4. The partner company or Mercari receives the item and ships it to the overseas buyer

Because the destination is in Japan, sellers do not need export packaging or export documents. The Mercari Help Center also asks sellers to ship each order separately, without combining multiple orders, to avoid mix-ups.

### Two Ways Overseas Buyers Reach Your Shop

There are two main channels through which overseas buyers purchase Mercari Shops items:

- **Official cross-border partners**: Partner companies take orders from overseas residents who cannot use Mercari and purchase items on their behalf. This was introduced to Mercari Shops in 2023. The Mercari Help Center lists 54 official partner companies (as of November 2026)
- **Mercari Global App**: Mercari's first worldwide app, which lets overseas buyers browse and buy items listed on Japan's Mercari and Mercari Shops. It launched in Taiwan and Hong Kong on September 30, 2025, and in the United States on June 17, 2026 (US time)

According to Mercari's press release, Mercari Shops sellers can sell to overseas buyers through the Global App without any additional settings or applications. Payments run through Stripe, delivery is handled with partners such as Sagawa Express, and the app offers real-time AI translation. Mercari's US launch announcement also highlights "full inspection," where Mercari checks every item before it ships, and free coverage for damage or loss in transit.

### Where Items Sell and What Sells

The official Mercari Shops website says cross-border sales reach 120 countries and regions, with China, the United States, and Taiwan as the top three destinations (as of November 2026). The same page reports that cross-border gross merchandise value doubled year over year for April 2025 to April 2026.

An official Mercari Shops article names "toys, hobbies, and goods," "fashion," and "books, magazines, and manga" as the categories with the most overseas orders.

## Mercari Shops Cross-Border Fees

Cross-border sales on Mercari Shops cost the same as domestic sales. An official Mercari Shops article states clearly that no extra shipping charges or fees apply to cross-border orders.

| Cost | Amount (as of November 2026) |
|---|---|
| Setup fee | Free |
| Monthly fee | Free |
| Sales fee | 10% of the tax-inclusive price (if shipping is charged separately, 10% of item price plus shipping) |
| Payout fee | 200 yen per payout |
| Extra fee for cross-border orders | None |
| International shipping and export | Not paid by the seller (handled by the partner or Mercari) |

The only shipping the seller pays for is delivery to the designated address in Japan. Any international shipping or service charges paid by the overseas buyer are handled on the partner's or Mercari's side and are separate from the seller's sales fee.

### Sample Fee Calculation

Here is a monthly estimate based on the published fees:

- Assumption: 20 items sold to overseas buyers in a month at 3,000 yen each (tax and shipping included)
- Sales: 3,000 yen x 20 = 60,000 yen
- Sales fee: 60,000 yen x 10% = 6,000 yen
- Payout fee: 200 yen (assuming one payout per month)
- Amount left for the seller: 53,800 yen (before domestic shipping and product costs)

With no fixed costs, a month with no sales costs nothing. That makes it an easy way to test overseas demand with a small number of items.

## How to Start Selling Overseas on Mercari Shops

To start, all you need to do is set your shop to accept orders from cross-border partners in the shop admin panel. According to an official Mercari Shops article, this setting is turned on ("Accept") by default.

1. Open a shop on Mercari Shops
2. In the shop admin panel, go to "Settings" and open "Edit shop information"
3. Under "Orders from cross-border EC partners," choose "Accept"
4. List your products and start selling

If you prefer not to sell cross-border, choose "Do not accept" in the same setting. (Menu names are translated from the Japanese admin panel.)

### Cancellations, Returns, and Eligible Products

An official Mercari Shops article explains how cancellations and returns work:

- **Cancellations**: The overseas buyer requests a cancellation through the partner company, and it is processed like a domestic cancellation
- **Returns**: The overseas buyer sends the item back to the partner company, which then returns it to the seller
- **Loss, damage, or disputes**: The partner company is the first point of contact

Only items that can be sold legally in Japan and are allowed under Mercari's guidelines can be sold. Items prohibited by the guidelines cannot be sold cross-border either.

## FAQ

**Q. Are there extra fees for cross-border sales on Mercari Shops?**

A. No. Cross-border orders carry the same 10% sales fee and 200 yen payout fee as domestic orders, and an official Mercari Shops article states that no additional shipping charges or fees apply (as of November 2026).

**Q. Do I have to ship overseas myself?**

A. No. You only ship to a designated address in Japan, and the partner company or Mercari handles international shipping, export, and customs.

**Q. Do I need to communicate in English or another foreign language?**

A. No. The partner company or Mercari handles communication with overseas buyers, and the Mercari Help Center notes that sellers can contact the official accounts in Japanese.

**Q. Can I turn off cross-border sales?**

A. Yes. In the shop admin panel, go to "Settings," open "Edit shop information," and choose "Do not accept" under "Orders from cross-border EC partners."

## Summary and Key Takeaway

Mercari Shops cross-border sales let you reach buyers in 120 countries and regions with the same shipping process and the same fees as domestic sales. As the Global App expands, more overseas buyers can purchase Mercari Shops items directly.

What cross-border sales add, however, is more chances to sell abroad, not a relationship with overseas customers. Because an intermediary handles buyer communication, sellers are left with little insight into who bought and few ways to bring them back. A practical approach is to validate overseas demand on Mercari Shops first, then expand the items that perform well into your own customer-building efforts, using each channel for what it does best.
