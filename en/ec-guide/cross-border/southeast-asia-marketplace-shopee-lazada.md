## Quick Overview: Choose Between Shopee and Lazada by Target Country and Shipping Model

If you want to sell to Southeast Asia from Japan, Shopee and Lazada are the two main cross-border marketplaces to consider. Shopee is the better fit if you also want to reach Taiwan or Brazil, while Lazada suits sellers who want to list in six Southeast Asian countries, including Indonesia, with a single product upload.

The two marketplaces also handle shipping differently. With Shopee, you ship directly from Japan to the buyer. With Lazada, you send each ordered item to a Lazada sorting center in Japan. On fees, Lazada's official Japan site states that commission is waived for the first 90 days and is 6–7% from day 91, while Shopee does not publish its commission rate on its public pages.

This article compares the target countries, fees, seller requirements, and onboarding steps of both marketplaces, based on what their official Japan seller sites say as of November 2026. It also includes a fee estimate and explains how to combine a marketplace with your own online store.

## What Are Southeast Asia Cross-Border Marketplaces?

Southeast Asia cross-border marketplaces are online shopping platforms where businesses in Japan can list and sell products from Japan to consumers in countries such as Singapore, Thailand, and Vietnam. They let you open a store without a local entity or local warehouse, which makes them a common first step into overseas sales.

Shopee and Lazada are the two major Southeast Asian marketplaces that Japanese businesses can join directly from Japan, and they are often compared. Both offer Japanese-language onboarding guides and Japanese-language seller support.

- **Shopee**: Operates marketplaces in Southeast Asia, Taiwan, and Brazil. Its Japan office is Shopee Japan K.K. According to Shopee's official Japan site, Shopee reached 10.9 billion orders and US$100.5 billion in gross merchandise value (GMV) in 2024 (figures for Shopee as a whole).
- **Lazada**: Founded in Singapore in 2012, Lazada became Alibaba Group's flagship ecommerce platform in Southeast Asia in 2016. According to its official Japan site, Lazada entered Japan in 2020 and began recruiting Japanese cross-border sellers.

## Shopee: Ship Directly from Japan to 7 Markets, Including Taiwan and Brazil

Shopee is a marketplace that lets you sell from Japan to seven markets: Singapore, Taiwan, Thailand, Malaysia, the Philippines, Vietnam, and Brazil. Shopee Japan's official site clearly states that it does not charge fixed costs such as setup fees or monthly fees.

- **Who can sell**: Not only companies but also individuals without a corporate number or a business registration filing can open a store.
- **Your first market**: New sellers can start in one of Singapore, Taiwan, Malaysia, or the Philippines. Selling in Thailand requires a separate application.
- **Shipping**: You ship directly from Japan to local buyers, not from a local warehouse. Orders must ship within 2 business days (3 for Taiwan, excluding Japanese weekends and holidays), and you can extend this by setting items as pre-orders.
- **Carriers**: You can choose from Shopee's own logistics service SLS (Singapore and the Philippines only), Japan Post, DHL, FedEx, Sagawa Express, Yamato Transport, and ECMS. Shopee also covers part of the international shipping cost (conditions apply).
- **Customer service language**: English for all markets except Taiwan, which uses Traditional Chinese. Markets where English is not an official language, such as Thailand, have an auto-translation feature.
- **Payouts**: Payoneer is the only supported payout method, with automatic deposits once a week.
- **Fees**: The public onboarding pages do not list commission rates; details are provided after you apply for an account.

Shopee suits sellers who want to test demand in Taiwan and Southeast Asia without fixed costs. For results Japanese sellers saw during a major sale, see our [article on Shopee's 11.11 Mega Day](/en/ec-guide/cross-border/shopee-1111-mega-day-cross-border-ec/).

## Lazada: List Once and Reach 6 Southeast Asian Countries

Lazada operates marketplaces in six countries: Singapore, Indonesia, Malaysia, Thailand, Vietnam, and the Philippines. According to its official Japan site (Lazada Japan), a single product upload lists your item in all six markets at once, and one contract gives you access to all six.

- **Who can sell**: Companies or sole proprietors who can ship from Japan or Hong Kong.
- **Fees**: Commission is waived for the first 90 days and is 6–7% from day 91.
- **Shipping**: When an order comes in, you send the item to a Lazada sorting center in Japan. Fulfillment services (storage and shipping on your behalf) and local bonded warehouses are also available.
- **Language**: English product descriptions are automatically translated into each local language, and the customer service chat has a translation feature.
- **Payouts**: The payout cycle is one week.
- **Support**: Japanese-language support is available, from product photos to shipping.
- **Recommended categories**: The official site lists Beauty, Health, Toys & Games, Watch Sunglasses, and Audio.

Lazada's cross-border program is called LazGlobal. Once you publish product information, it syncs to Lazada's sites in all six countries. The case studies on the official site feature companies selling from Japan, such as want.jp, Laox, and Kohnan Shoji.

Lazada suits sellers who want to list across six Southeast Asian countries at once, including Indonesia and Thailand.

## How to Open a Store on Shopee and Lazada

On both Shopee and Lazada, you start by applying for an account on the official Japan site, then set up your shop and list products after review.

### Opening a Shopee Store (4 Steps)

1. **Apply for an account**: Fill out the application form on the official site. Companies enter their corporate number, and individuals can optionally submit a copy of their business registration filing. You choose one market to start.
2. **Open your shop account**: After review, follow the instructions in the email that arrives within a few days.
3. **Set up your shop**: In Seller Centre (the seller dashboard), enter your basic information and the shop profile buyers will see.
4. **List products**: Upload at least five products to complete store setup. You must list at least five products within 30 days of applying.

You can add products one at a time, upload them in bulk with an Excel file, or connect an external management system via API.

### Opening a Lazada Store (4 Steps)

1. **Apply for an account**: Click "Apply for an account" on the official site and register in Seller Center as a Japanese cross-border seller.
2. **Request approval**: Review the application guide on the official site, then email Lazada to request account approval. Approval takes about one week.
3. **Prepare your store**: Get everything ready to start selling.
4. **Start selling**: Begin selling on Lazada.

## How to Choose Between Shopee and Lazada

Comparing Shopee and Lazada in the following four steps makes the decision easier.

1. **Choose by target country**: Shopee if you want to sell to Taiwan or Brazil, Lazada if you want to sell to Indonesia. Singapore, Malaysia, the Philippines, Thailand, and Vietnam are covered by both.
2. **Choose by business type**: If you are an individual without a corporate number or business registration filing, Shopee is the option. Lazada is for companies and sole proprietors.
3. **Choose by shipping model**: Shopee if you want to pick a carrier and ship each order directly, Lazada if you prefer sending orders to a sorting center in Japan.
4. **Choose by upfront cost**: Shopee has no fixed costs, and Lazada waives commission for the first 90 days. For both, check the latest terms when you apply.

Some sellers use both. You can also start with one marketplace to find out which products and countries work, then expand to the second. ## When to Use a Marketplace vs. Your Own Store

A cross-border marketplace and your own store are not an either-or choice. In practice, it works best to give each a different role and use them together.

- **When a marketplace fits**: You want to test whether your products sell in Southeast Asia first, you want to tap into the reach of marketplace sales and campaigns, or you do not have staff for marketing.
- **When your own store fits**: You want to convey your brand's world, build repeat customers, or deal directly with business buyers overseas.

On a marketplace, buyer information and storefront design are handled within the marketplace's rules. Once you see which countries respond well on a marketplace, you can build your own store for those countries and grow repeat sales through email newsletters and other channels.

## FAQ

**Q. Should I start with Shopee or Lazada?**

A. The basic rule is to decide by the countries you want to sell to. Shopee fits if you also want to reach Taiwan or Brazil, while Lazada fits if you want to list across six Southeast Asian countries at once, including Indonesia. If you want to start as an individual, Shopee is the option, since you can open a store without a corporate number or business registration filing.

**Q. Can I sell on Shopee or Lazada without a local entity?**

A. Yes, both let you sell from Japan as a cross-border seller. With Shopee, you ship directly from Japan to the buyer; with Lazada, you send items to a Lazada sorting center in Japan.

**Q. How much are Shopee's and Lazada's fees?**

A. Lazada's official Japan site states that commission is waived for the first 90 days and is 6–7% from day 91 (as of November 2026). Shopee charges no fixed costs such as setup or monthly fees, but its commission rate is not listed on its public pages and is provided after you apply for an account.

**Q. Can I open a store if I don't speak English well?**

A. Both marketplaces offer Japanese-language support and translation features. Shopee handles customer service in English for all markets except Taiwan, with auto-translation for markets where English is not an official language. Lazada automatically translates English product descriptions into each local language.

## Summary and Key Takeaway

Shopee and Lazada both let you sell from Japan to Southeast Asia, but they differ in target countries, shipping model, and how openly they publish fees. Shopee ships directly from Japan to seven markets, including Taiwan and Brazil, and accepts individual sellers. Lazada lets you list in six countries, including Indonesia, with a single upload, and you only need to send orders to a sorting center in Japan.

The comparison shows that the answer depends less on which is cheaper and more on which countries' buyers you want to reach. Start with one marketplace to find the countries and products that sell, then build relationships with customers through your own store in the countries where you see traction. Following this order lets you expand into Southeast Asia at a manageable pace while keeping an eye on fees and marketing effort.
