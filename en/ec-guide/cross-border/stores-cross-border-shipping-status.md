## Quick Overview: STORES Can Sell Overseas Through Its "Overseas Sales Integration"

If you're running a domestic STORES Netshop and looking into whether you can sell overseas, you'll quickly run into the "Overseas Sales Integration" ("海外販売連携") that launched on July 7, 2026. The short answer: STORES now connects with the cross-border purchase-proxy service WorldShopping BIZ, run by Zigzag Inc., letting you sell to 228 countries and regions while keeping your domestic shipping and operations largely unchanged. This guide covers what the integration includes, its cost, language support, and what's still not confirmed by official sources.

## What the Overseas Sales Integration Covers

Per STORES Inc.'s press release, the Overseas Sales Integration launched on July 7, 2026, connecting STORES Netshop with WorldShopping BIZ. Participating shops just ship products to a designated domestic warehouse — WorldShopping BIZ handles arranging international shipping, packaging, and customs clearance. Customer support for overseas buyers is handled through an inquiry desk that operates 365 days a year in Japanese, English, Chinese (Traditional and Simplified), and Korean.

## How the Cost Works

WorldShopping BIZ normally charges an initial setup fee of ¥33,000 (tax included) and a monthly fee of ¥5,500 (tax included), but the press release states both fees are waived when signing up through STORES Netshop. Your regular STORES Netshop plan fee still applies separately. Overseas buyers are charged a 10% purchase-proxy fee on top of the product price and domestic shipping cost — this isn't a cost the shop itself bears. Since WorldShopping BIZ also handles collecting payment from overseas customers, the press release notes that shops don't carry the risk of currency fluctuation or uncollected payments.

## How Far Does English Support Actually Go?

Here's the detail worth paying attention to: the Overseas Sales Integration handles multilingual support at the point of purchase and customer inquiries — it isn't a feature that lets the shop itself translate its own STORES admin or storefront into English. As of this writing, we couldn't confirm from official sources whether STORES Netshop has its own feature, comparable to Shopify's "add a store language" setting, for shops to prepare their own multilingual storefront pages. In other words, the "English support" available right now works through WorldShopping BIZ's own multilingual purchase interface for overseas buyers — it doesn't mean the shop's own storefront pages become multilingual.

## How to Set It Up

Per the press release, the setup process is:

1. Apply through the WorldShopping BIZ application form
2. Receive a token issued by WorldShopping BIZ
3. Paste that token into the "Overseas Sales Integration" settings page in the STORES Netshop admin and save it
4. Notify WorldShopping BIZ that setup is complete, confirm it works, and start using the integration

Note that some products and services can't be handled through this integration, and sellers are advised to check WorldShopping BIZ's FAQ before applying.

## What's Still Missing

The Overseas Sales Integration is a solid first step for cross-border selling, since it offloads the operational burden of overseas shipping, payment, and multilingual support. What it doesn't cover is the marketing side of things — writing overseas-focused SEO content, running email marketing aimed at overseas customers, or localizing your content for a specific target country — in other words, the work of getting your shop found by overseas customers in the first place. ## FAQ

**Q. Does STORES Netshop support shipping overseas?**

A. Yes — through the Overseas Sales Integration launched on July 7, 2026, shops just ship to a designated domestic warehouse while WorldShopping BIZ handles international shipping and customs, reaching 228 countries and regions.

**Q. Does using the Overseas Sales Integration cost anything?**

A. Signing up through STORES Netshop waives the ¥33,000 setup fee and ¥5,500 monthly fee (both tax included) that WorldShopping BIZ normally charges. Overseas buyers separately pay a 10% purchase-proxy fee on the product price and domestic shipping.

**Q. Can I make my STORES Netshop storefront itself available in English?**

A. As of this writing, we couldn't confirm an official feature letting shops translate their own storefront pages into multiple languages. The English support currently available works mainly through WorldShopping BIZ's own multilingual purchase interface.

**Q. Does the Overseas Sales Integration help with marketing to overseas customers?**

A. No — the integration itself covers shipping, payment, and multilingual customer support. It doesn't include marketing features like overseas SEO content or email marketing.

## Summary and Key Takeaway

STORES's Overseas Sales Integration handles the shipping, payment, and multilingual support hurdles that typically come up first in cross-border selling, and it does so at a low upfront cost. What's outside its current scope, based on what's officially confirmed, is translating the storefront itself and marketing to overseas customers.
