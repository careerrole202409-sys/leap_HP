## Quick Overview: How to Sell Internationally on Rakuten Ichiba

As of November 2026, Rakuten Ichiba merchants have three main ways to sell to customers outside Japan. The first is to let overseas customers order through Rakuten Global Express, Rakuten's official package forwarding service. The second is to arrange international shipping yourself and send orders directly abroad. The third is to sell through a cross-border proxy shopping service such as SAZO.

Rakuten Global Market, the overseas storefront that Rakuten Ichiba merchants once used, was reported to have closed in June 2020. Today there is no official way to turn a Rakuten Ichiba store into a multilingual store for overseas shoppers. Instead, merchants combine services that take care of shipping or purchasing on their behalf.

Key points to watch: Rakuten Ichiba has temporarily stopped accepting orders from some countries, certain items (such as products with lithium batteries and alcohol of 25% ABV or more) cannot be shipped abroad, and buyers may be charged import duties and taxes in the destination country. Whichever method you choose, you also need a separate plan for getting overseas shoppers to find you. This article explains how each method works, what to watch out for, and the option of running your own cross-border online store, based on Rakuten's official information.

## What Selling Overseas on Rakuten Ichiba Means

Selling overseas on Rakuten Ichiba means that a merchant with a Rakuten Ichiba store sells and delivers products to customers who live outside Japan. Because Rakuten Ichiba stores are built for shoppers in Japan, selling to overseas customers requires something that fills the gap in international shipping, payment, or language.

Searches for "Rakuten Ichiba cross-border ecommerce" also bring up many stories about overseas businesses opening stores on Rakuten Ichiba to sell to Japanese consumers. This article focuses on the opposite direction: how Japanese merchants can sell from Rakuten Ichiba to customers abroad.

## Rakuten Global Market Closed in 2020

Rakuten Global Market was a site where Rakuten Ichiba merchants could sell their listed products to overseas customers, but it was reported to have closed on June 29, 2020. According to a Nihon Net Keizai Shimbun article dated April 30, 2020, Rakuten explained that it was ending the service "to focus on businesses with higher growth potential," and said it would put more effort into its "Overseas Partner Flagship Store" business, which sells Japanese products through partnerships with overseas ecommerce marketplaces.

Some older articles still describe Rakuten Global Market as an active service. When researching how to sell overseas on Rakuten Ichiba, check the publication date and confirm the current details on Rakuten's official sites.

## Three Ways to Sell Overseas on Rakuten Ichiba

Rakuten Ichiba merchants can currently sell overseas in the following three ways.

1. **Orders through Rakuten Global Express**: Overseas customers order using Rakuten's official forwarding service, and the store ships to a warehouse in Japan.
2. **Shipping directly abroad**: The store decides whether it ships internationally, which carrier to use, and how much to charge, and sends orders abroad itself.
3. **Orders through a proxy shopping service**: A cross-border proxy shopping service places the order on behalf of the overseas customer, and the store ships to the service's base in Japan.

In all three cases, the Rakuten Ichiba store itself stays geared to Japan, and other services fill in shipping or purchasing. Let's look at each one.

## How Rakuten Global Express Works

Rakuten Global Express is Rakuten's official package forwarding service that delivers products bought on Japanese online stores to customers overseas. As of November 2026, its official site lists 78 countries and regions as available destinations.

### How Overseas Customers Use It

The Rakuten Global Express website describes the customer flow as follows.

1. Log in with a Rakuten user ID and get a personal "shopping address" on My Page (the address of the customer's own storage box at the warehouse in Kawasaki, Kanagawa).
2. Shop on Rakuten Ichiba and enter the shopping address as the delivery address.
3. Request packing for parcels that have arrived at the warehouse (multiple parcels can be combined into one shipment).
4. Choose a shipping method, request shipment, and pay the international shipping fee with the credit card registered to the Rakuten ID or with Rakuten Points.
5. Receive the parcel at the designated overseas address (import duties may apply depending on the destination and the contents).

According to the site, customers must complete identity verification under Japan's Act on Prevention of Transfer of Criminal Proceeds.

### What the Merchant Needs to Do

From the merchant's side, an order placed through Rakuten Global Express is a normal order whose delivery address happens to be a warehouse in Japan. The store simply ships to the Kawasaki warehouse as it would any domestic order. International shipping and packing are handled between the customer and Rakuten Global Express.

That said, the official site states that payment methods such as cash on delivery, carrier-collected payment, and e-Collect cannot be used. It also does not accept refrigerated or frozen items, and anything sent that way is stored and shipped at room temperature. Stores selling food or similar products can avoid misunderstandings by noting temperature requirements on their product pages.

### Example International Shipping Costs

Customers pay the international shipping fee to Rakuten Global Express. The official site gives examples such as four bottles of shampoo (1,790 g) shipped to Hong Kong by Economy Air for 4,580 yen, and four CDs (900 g) shipped to the United States by Super Express for 5,200 yen (examples shown as of November 2026; actual fees vary by parcel and timing).

Because shipping costs of this level are added to the product price, the service suits customers who buy several items together better than those buying a single low-priced item.

## What to Watch Out For When Shipping Directly Abroad

Rakuten Ichiba's help center explains that whether international shipping is available, which carrier is used, and how items are shipped all vary by store. In other words, shipping directly abroad is each store's own decision.

The most important point for direct shipping is that Rakuten Ichiba has temporarily stopped accepting orders from some countries. According to Rakuten Ichiba's help center, changes to tax systems such as value-added tax (VAT) and goods and services tax (GST) may make local delivery and customs clearance difficult, so orders from countries including the United States, the United Kingdom, Germany, France, Australia, Singapore, Thailand, and Malaysia are temporarily suspended (as of November 2026). The same page notes that Rakuten Global Express may still be able to deliver to some of these countries.

Rakuten Ichiba's help center also explains the following.

- International shipping fees depend on the carrier, size, and weight, so the store provides the exact amount after the order is placed.
- Buyers may be charged import duties and taxes in the destination country.
- If an item is confiscated by customs, most stores do not issue refunds.

Stores that ship directly abroad can reduce inquiries and disputes by publishing a page, readable before ordering, that explains how shipping fees are confirmed, who pays duties, and what happens if an item is confiscated.

## Selling Through a Proxy Shopping Service

Selling through a proxy shopping service means that a cross-border ecommerce company buys Rakuten Ichiba products on behalf of overseas customers and ships them abroad from its own warehouse.

One officially announced partnership with Rakuten Ichiba is SAZO, a cross-border ecommerce service for South Korea. According to SAZO's press release of April 14, 2026, a product information API connection lets Korean users browse Rakuten Ichiba product listings translated into Korean on SAZO and buy them there. Rakuten Ichiba merchants ship the ordered items to SAZO's base in Japan, and SAZO inspects, packs, and delivers them overseas. SAZO also handles international shipping procedures and customer inquiries, and runs joint promotions with Rakuten Ichiba, such as issuing coupons.

For merchants, the benefit is that orders reach Korean customers while the shipping work stays the same as for domestic orders. However, the press release does not mention merchant fees or participation requirements, so check the details with each service.

## Key Cautions When Selling Overseas on Rakuten Ichiba

Whichever method you choose, keep the following points in mind when selling overseas on Rakuten Ichiba.

### 1. Some Items Cannot Be Shipped Abroad

The Rakuten Global Express website lists items that require caution because they cannot be shipped abroad, including true wireless earbuds and power banks with lithium batteries, alcohol of 25% ABV or more, perfume, nail polish, aerosol cans, and frozen or refrigerated goods. Each country and region also has its own restricted items. Check early whether your main products can be shipped.

### 2. Destinations and Shipping Methods Change

As the list of countries Rakuten Ichiba has suspended shows, changes in tax rules and national regulations can change where and how you can ship. Rakuten Global Express also posted a notice on November 19, 2026, about suspending some shipping methods to Germany. If you plan to keep selling overseas, check Rakuten's help pages and notices regularly.

### 3. It Is Hard to Build Relationships With Overseas Customers

For orders placed through Rakuten Global Express or a proxy shopping service, the delivery address the store sees is a warehouse in Japan or the proxy company's base. This makes it difficult for the store to know which country a customer is in or what they bought, and to turn that into repeat purchases.

### 4. Overseas Marketing Has to Be Planned Separately

Rakuten Global Express is a shipping service, and its official description does not include any features for promoting stores to overseas shoppers. Some proxy shopping services run promotions, such as SAZO's Korean campaigns, but this varies by country and service. If you want to grow overseas sales, you need a separate way for overseas shoppers to find your products.

## FAQ

**Q. Can I sell my Rakuten Ichiba products to overseas customers?**

A. Yes. You can have customers order through Rakuten Global Express, Rakuten's official forwarding service, ship directly abroad from your store, or sell through a proxy shopping service such as SAZO.

**Q. Is Rakuten Global Market still available?**

A. No. Rakuten Global Market was reported to have closed on June 29, 2020. Today, merchants mainly combine shipping and purchasing services such as Rakuten Global Express.

**Q. What does a merchant need to do for orders through Rakuten Global Express?**

A. Simply ship to the Kawasaki warehouse address on the order, just like a domestic order. Packing, international shipping requests, and shipping payment are handled between the customer and Rakuten Global Express. Note that cash on delivery and carrier-collected payment cannot be used, and refrigerated and frozen items are not accepted.

**Q. Are there countries Rakuten Ichiba does not accept overseas orders from?**

A. Yes. According to Rakuten Ichiba's help center, orders from countries including the United States, the United Kingdom, Germany, Australia, and Singapore are temporarily suspended due to changes in tax systems such as VAT and GST (as of November 2026). Rakuten Global Express may still be able to deliver to these countries.

**Q. Who pays import duties when selling overseas?**

A. Rakuten Ichiba's help center states that buyers may be charged import duties and taxes in the destination country. Amounts and conditions vary by country, so it helps to tell customers about this on your product pages.

## Summary and Key Takeaway

There are three ways to sell overseas on Rakuten Ichiba: Rakuten Global Express, shipping directly, and proxy shopping services. Now that Rakuten Global Market has closed, all three work the same way: the Rakuten Ichiba store stays geared to Japan while other services fill in shipping or purchasing. They are easy to start, but come with cautions such as items that cannot be shipped, suspended countries, and import duties, and available destinations and methods can change with regulations.

The key takeaway is that overseas sales on Rakuten Ichiba are a way to respond to orders that come from abroad, not a way to grow your overseas customer base yourself. Start by testing overseas demand with your usual shipping routine, and if you see traction, add your own cross-border store, where customer information stays with you and you can design overseas marketing. Treating Rakuten Ichiba and your own cross-border store as channels with different roles makes the next step easier to choose.
