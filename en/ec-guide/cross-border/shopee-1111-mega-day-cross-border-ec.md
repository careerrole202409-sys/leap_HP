## Quick Overview: GMV Jumped 24x in the First Four Minutes

If you're researching "Shopee 11.11" or "cross-border sale," you probably want concrete numbers on just how much a major annual sale can move cross-border sales. On November 19, 2026, Shopee Japan announced that during its biggest annual sale, "11.11 Mega Day," Japanese cross-border sellers' gross merchandise value (GMV) in the first four minutes hit 24 times the average for the same time slot in Q2 2026. This guide breaks down what the announcement actually says, and how to think about riding a seasonal sale in cross-border e-commerce.

## The "11.11 Mega Day" Numbers

According to Shopee Japan's announcement, here's what Japanese cross-border sellers saw on November 11, 2026, across seven markets (five Southeast Asian markets, Taiwan, and Brazil):

- GMV in the first 4 minutes: 24x the average for the same time slot in Q2 2026
- Orders in the first 4 minutes: 21x the same-period average
- Full-day GMV: 5x the Q2 2026 daily average

By market, Taiwan's GMV in the first two hours hit 17x the same-period average, and Vietnam's order count in the same window hit 23x. By category, both beauty and sports/outdoor GMV reached 6x the Q2 daily average, with additional demand in toys/collectibles, health products, and stationery. Top-ordered categories varied by country: makeup and skincare in Vietnam, sports and leisure gear in Malaysia, and toys/games in Brazil. Orders coming through Shopee Live from Japanese cross-border sellers reached 9x the Q2 daily average.

One thing worth keeping in mind: these multiples are compared against the same-period average in Q2 2026, not the same period a year earlier. A sale-day spike and steady year-round growth are two different things.

## Why Demand Concentrates Around Sale Days

In markets like Southeast Asia, Taiwan, and Brazil, a date like "11.11" has become something of a fixed "stock-up day" for shoppers. The sharp jump in orders coming through Shopee Live suggests that what drives actual orders on sale day isn't just discounting — it's the combination of live-streamed selling and promotional push that drives real purchase intent. For a Japan-based business selling cross-border into Southeast Asia, lining up inventory, shipping capacity, and customer support ahead of these known sale dates is a real opportunity to capture demand.

## Marketplace vs. Owned Site: Different Ways to Approach a Sale

Selling on a marketplace like Shopee means you can ride the marketplace's own promotional pull during a sale like this — the kind of result shown above is a clear example of how fast that can pay off. The tradeoff is that your sales depend heavily on the marketplace's own traffic, and it's harder to build a direct relationship with your customer data.

Running your own cross-border site doesn't give you that same one-off traffic boost, but it comes with its own advantages: you own your customer data directly, and you can set country, language, and currency flexibly per product. ## FAQ

**Q. What is Shopee's "11.11 Mega Day"?**

A. It's Shopee's biggest annual sale event, held every November 11. For 2026, Shopee Japan reported a sharp jump in results for Japanese cross-border sellers across seven markets: five Southeast Asian countries, Taiwan, and Brazil.

**Q. Is the "24x GMV" figure compared to last year?**

A. No. Per Shopee Japan's announcement, that 24x figure is compared to the average for the same time slot in Q2 2026, not the same period a year earlier.

**Q. Can an owned cross-border site benefit from a sale like this too?**

A. Not with the same one-off traffic boost a marketplace gets, but you can aim for a similar effect by ramping up ads and email campaigns around the sale window, and building country-specific campaign pages. Pairing that with the customer data you build on your own site is key.

**Q. For Southeast Asia, is a marketplace or an owned site the better choice for cross-border selling?**

A. A marketplace suits sellers who want fast traffic in the short term; an owned site suits those building a brand and customer data over the long term. Many businesses use both, choosing based on their products and operational capacity.
