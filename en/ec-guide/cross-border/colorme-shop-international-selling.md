## Quick Overview: Color Me Shop Stores Can Start Selling Overseas with the Built-in "Overseas Sales Proxy"

The easiest way to sell internationally on Color Me Shop (カラーミーショップ, a Japanese ecommerce platform run by GMO Pepabo) is the built-in "Overseas Sales Proxy" (海外販売代行) feature, included in every plan since July 22, 2025. A purchase banner appears only to visitors from outside Japan, and the proxy company ZenGroup handles the order, payment, international shipping and customer inquiries. The store simply ships the item to a warehouse in Osaka, just like a domestic order, at no extra cost.

There are two other routes: installing an overseas sales app, or shipping abroad yourself. What Color Me Shop does not offer out of the box is a way to translate your storefront or to market it to overseas shoppers. This article lays out the three methods, their costs, the current state of multilingual support and how to fill the gaps, based on official information as of November 2026.

## Can You Sell Overseas on Color Me Shop?

Yes. Color Me Shop's official help page "Can I sell overseas?" points to two options: the Overseas Sales Proxy feature and the overseas sales apps in the Color Me Shop App Store. In practice, there are three methods.

1. **Use the built-in Overseas Sales Proxy**: no extra cost; you ship to a warehouse in Japan
2. **Install an overseas sales app**: for stores that want extras such as marketing support or analytics
3. **Ship abroad yourself**: you arrange international shipping and payments without a proxy

Methods 1 and 2 are "proxy purchase" models: a proxy company buys the item in Japan on behalf of the overseas customer and ships it abroad. From the store's side, it looks exactly like a domestic order, so no English support or customs paperwork is required. Method 3 takes more work, but you deal directly with overseas customers.

## Method 1: The Built-in Overseas Sales Proxy

The Overseas Sales Proxy is a standard Color Me Shop feature that uses ZenGroup Inc.'s cross-border service "ZenLink" to handle orders, payment, international shipping and inquiries from overseas customers on the store's behalf.

### How the Overseas Sales Proxy Works

According to the official help center, the flow looks like this:

1. When someone visits the store from abroad, a banner in the visitor's language appears automatically on the homepage and product pages. Visitors in Japan do not see it.
2. The shopper clicks through to an overseas purchase page (ZenMarket), where they place the order and pay.
3. ZenGroup reviews the order and buys the item from the store like any regular customer. This is when the order appears in the store's system.
4. The store ships the item as usual to the ZenGroup warehouse address in Osaka shown on the order.
5. ZenGroup handles reinforced packing, the invoice, international shipping and customs.

The overseas purchase page supports 19 languages and more than 150 payment methods, including credit cards, PayPal, Alipay and bank transfer. ZenGroup's customer support handles inquiries from overseas shoppers around the clock.

### What the Overseas Sales Proxy Costs

The Overseas Sales Proxy is free to use, with no setup fees, sales commissions or other added charges. The overseas customer pays the international shipping and ZenLink's service fee.

ZenGroup pays domestic shipping to its warehouse based on the store's normal shipping settings. If your shipping fee is set at 700 yen, ZenGroup pays 700 yen; if orders over 10,000 yen ship free, an order above that amount comes in with free shipping. Your usual payment processing fees still apply, just as with domestic sales.

### Turning It On or Off

The feature is enabled automatically for any store with an active contract that is not using another overseas sales app. To opt out, go to "Settings > Overseas Sales > Overseas Sales Proxy" (設定＞海外販売＞海外販売代行) in the admin panel and switch it to "Do not display" (表示しない). Stores in their free trial or already using an overseas sales app have it turned off from the start.

### Stores That Cannot Use It and Items That Cannot Ship Abroad

ZenGroup screens stores before the feature goes live. If a store does not pass, the setting switches to "Do not display" automatically, and the result is shown on the admin dashboard and the Overseas Sales Proxy page. The official help center lists these examples of stores that may not pass:

- Stores that accept none of credit card, Amazon Pay, bank transfer or cash on delivery
- Stores where roughly half or more of the products cannot be shipped abroad
- Stores that require email or phone contact to complete a purchase
- Stores that ship from outside Japan
- Stores selling on an external site through the Cart JS feature (Dokodemo Color Me)

Items that cannot be shipped abroad include refrigerated or frozen goods, downloadable products, subscription items, products with less than 60 days of shelf life, oversized items (roughly over 1 meter per side, 4 meters in girth or 30 kg when packed), flammable or hazardous goods such as aerosol cans, and high-proof alcohol. The built-in feature has no setting to exclude specific products. If you receive an order for something you do not want to send abroad, contact ZenGroup and then cancel the order.

## Method 2: Install an Overseas Sales App

Overseas sales apps are proxy-purchase cross-border services offered in the Color Me Shop App Store. As with the built-in feature, you ship to a warehouse in Japan, and depending on the app you also get marketing support, an analytics dashboard or product-level sales controls. None of these apps can be used together with the Overseas Sales Proxy or with each other.

| App | Provider | Cost to the store | Key features |
|---|---|---|---|
| ZenLink for Color Me Shop | ZenGroup | No setup or monthly fee; 10% of overseas sales via ZenLink (tax included, excluding shipping) | 19 languages, 150+ payment methods, per-product sales settings, dashboard showing sales by country, promotion on partner sites |
| Buyee Connect Free | BEENOS Solutions | No setup, monthly or sales fees | Overseas cart and checkout in 19 languages; ships to about 120 countries and regions; buyers pay a 500 yen fee per order |
| Buyee Connect Pro for Color Me Shop | BEENOS Solutions | 5,500 yen/month (tax included); no sales fee | Everything in Free, plus buyer analytics, coupons and sales controls by country and product |
| WorldShopping BIZ for Color Me Shop | ZIGZAG | 5,500 yen/month (tax included); 33,000 yen setup fee (tax included) waived for Color Me Shop users for a limited time | Sells to 228 countries and regions; multilingual navigation, 24/7 multilingual support, dashboard |

(From each app's Color Me Shop App Store page, as of November 2026)

The official listing describes the ZenLink app as aimed at stores whose sales did not grow with the free built-in feature, or that did not pass its screening. Once installed, the app takes priority over the built-in feature, and every order placed through ZenLink incurs the 10% service fee.

## Method 3: Ship Abroad Yourself

If you want to ship internationally without a proxy, use the "Overseas Shipping Assist" (海外配送アシスト) app. It is an official Color Me Shop app that lets you manage the information needed for overseas shipping from the admin panel.

- Register an English product name, English material name and HS code (the international product classification number) for each product.
- Set which countries each shipping method can deliver to (for example, limiting a domestic-only carrier like Yamato Transport to Japan).
- Add a "Country" field to the address form and accept overseas addresses in half-width alphanumeric characters.

The app costs 5,500 yen per month (tax included) on plans such as Regular and Free, and is free on plans such as Large and Premium. Color Me Shop says PayPal can be connected for payments. However, according to the official help center, the cart cannot display currencies other than Japanese yen, nor can it be shown in other languages. You arrange international shipping yourself by contracting services such as EMS or international couriers (see our [overseas carrier comparison](/en/ec-guide/cross-border/overseas-carrier-comparison-dhl-fedex-ems/) for how to choose).

## Multilingual Support on Color Me Shop

Color Me Shop has no built-in feature for translating your storefront. The official help page "Do you support multiple languages?" points to three options.

| Option | What gets translated | Caveats |
|---|---|---|
| The "shutto Translation" (shutto翻訳) app | Store pages such as the homepage and product pages (100+ languages) | Secure pages such as the cart, member login and contact form are not translated |
| Editing the template HTML/CSS yourself | Pages you edit | Requires technical skills and is not covered by support or guarantees; secure pages cannot be edited |
| WorldShopping BIZ for Color Me Shop | Cart page | Whether the payment screen is translated depends on your payment provider |

The official help center notes that most overseas shoppers use the automatic translation in browsers such as Google Chrome, so translating your store is not essential if you use the Overseas Sales Proxy.

## Cost Estimate: What a Store Pays on 100,000 Yen in Monthly Overseas Sales

Here is an estimate of the store's additional monthly cost if overseas customers order 100,000 yen worth of goods (tax included, excluding shipping), based on official pricing as of November 2026 (normal payment processing fees excluded).

| Method | Additional cost to the store (per month) |
|---|---|
| Overseas Sales Proxy (built-in) | 0 yen |
| Buyee Connect Free | 0 yen |
| Buyee Connect Pro | 5,500 yen |
| WorldShopping BIZ | 5,500 yen |
| ZenLink for Color Me Shop | 10,000 yen (10% of 100,000 yen) |

In terms of cost to the store, the built-in Overseas Sales Proxy is the lightest option. Paid apps make sense if you expect enough extra sales to justify the fee. Keep in mind that with proxy purchase models, overseas customers pay shipping and the proxy's fees, so their total cost is higher than your listed price.

## How to Fill the Gaps in Overseas Selling

The biggest gap in selling overseas on Color Me Shop is marketing: getting overseas shoppers to find your store in the first place. The Overseas Sales Proxy lets visitors from abroad buy, but it does not bring in that traffic. Color Me Shop's help center itself recommends announcing on social media that overseas customers can now buy from you.

Depending on your goals, there are three ways to fill the gap:

1. **Use the proxy services' marketing**: the ZenLink and Buyee apps put your products in front of their overseas members through listings on partner proxy sites and coupons.
2. **Translate your store pages and get them into search**: shutto Translation includes international SEO features that make translated pages easier to surface in overseas search.
3. **Run a separate overseas site**: keep Color Me Shop for Japan and use a multilingual, multi-currency site for overseas customers, publishing articles and newsletters in their language.

Starting with the built-in feature to see which countries and products get traction, then moving on to options 2 or 3, keeps wasted effort to a minimum.

## FAQ

**Q. Does Color Me Shop support overseas shipping?**

A. Yes. With the built-in Overseas Sales Proxy, the store only ships to a warehouse in Japan, and the proxy company ZenGroup takes care of international shipping and customs. If you want to ship directly yourself, the Overseas Shipping Assist app lets you set shipping methods by country and register English product names and HS codes.

**Q. Are there fees for Color Me Shop's Overseas Sales Proxy?**

A. There are no fees for the store. Setup and sales commissions are free, and the overseas customer pays international shipping and the service fee. However, if you install the ZenLink for Color Me Shop app, 10% of sales through ZenLink (tax included, excluding shipping) is charged as a service fee.

**Q. Can I make my Color Me Shop store English?**

A. Not with the built-in features. Store pages can be translated with the shutto Translation app, and the cart page is translated by WorldShopping BIZ for Color Me Shop. You can also edit the template HTML/CSS yourself, but secure pages such as the cart cannot be edited.

**Q. What does the store need to do when an overseas order comes in?**

A. Just ship it like a domestic order. With the Overseas Sales Proxy, ZenGroup places the order as a regular customer, so you ship to the warehouse address on the order, including the identification ID at the end of the address. Refer any inquiries from overseas customers to ZenGroup's support team.

**Q. Can I exclude products I don't want to sell through the Overseas Sales Proxy?**

A. Not with the built-in feature, which has no setting to exclude specific products. If you get an order for an item you don't want to send abroad, contact ZenGroup and then cancel it. To control sales product by product, use the ZenLink for Color Me Shop app.

## Summary and Key Takeaway

Thanks to the built-in Overseas Sales Proxy, Color Me Shop stores can start selling overseas with no added cost and no English support needed. Stores that want stronger marketing or analytics can move to an overseas sales app, and those that want to deal directly with customers can use Overseas Shipping Assist, so there are options for each stage.

At the same time, every one of these methods only lets visitors from abroad buy; you still need to create the reasons for overseas shoppers to find your store. A practical approach is to split overseas selling into two jobs, "being able to sell" and "being found," let Color Me Shop's built-in feature handle the first, and put your time and money into the second.
