## Quick Overview: Choose a Taiwan Marketplace by Whether You Can Sell Directly from Japan

When picking a marketplace for selling to Taiwan, the fastest first step is to check whether a Japanese company can open a store there directly. Among Taiwan's leading marketplaces, Shopee has a dedicated cross-border program for Japanese businesses (run through Shopee Japan), and both companies and individuals can sell directly from Japan. momo and PChome 24h, on the other hand, are major marketplaces built for Taiwan's domestic market. As of November 2026, the official pages we checked do not publish seller requirements or fees for overseas businesses.

This article is for businesses that want to sell to Taiwan. We compare momo, PChome 24h, and Shopee on their key features and how to get listed, based on official information. ## Taiwan's Major Online Marketplaces

Taiwan's three major online marketplaces are momo, PChome 24h, and Shopee. Most of the top-ranking guides on this topic feature these three as Taiwan's leading shopping sites, and many also mention Yahoo Kimo Shopping Center (the Taiwanese version of Yahoo! Shopping).

The three marketplaces are run in very different ways:

- **momo (momo購物網)**: A major marketplace focused on B2C (business-to-consumer) sales, operated by Taiwan's Fubon Media Technology
- **PChome 24h (PChome 24h購物)**: A major B2C-focused marketplace operated by Taiwan's PChome Online
- **Shopee (蝦皮購物)**: A marketplace where many independent stores sell, operated by Shopee, which was founded in Singapore. It has a cross-border program for sellers in Japan

## Features and How to Get Listed on Each Marketplace

### momo: A Major B2C Marketplace with Over 10 Million Members

momo is an online marketplace that Fubon Media Technology launched in May 2005. The company was founded in September 2004 and listed on the Taiwan Stock Exchange in December 2014. Its official website describes momo as Taiwan's largest B2C shopping site by GMV (gross merchandise value) and says it serves more than 10 million members (as of November 2026).

momo stands out for its extensive logistics network. It operates fulfillment centers in northern, central, and southern Taiwan, and in 2021 it launched "5h超市," a service that delivers within five hours. The company also runs TV shopping and catalog sales, and in March 2024 it launched "mo店+," a service where independent stores can open shops.

On how to get listed, momo's corporate website and its store management portal do not publish requirements or fees for overseas sellers. According to a 2017 report by JETRO (Japan External Trade Organization), selling on momo required a local corporate registration in Taiwan, and payments were settled only within Taiwan in New Taiwan dollars. For a Japanese business, the realistic routes are to have a Taiwanese importer or wholesale partner list your products, or to set up a Taiwanese entity and contact momo's merchant recruitment desk.

### PChome 24h: A Long-Running B2C Marketplace Known for 24-Hour Delivery

PChome 24h is a B2C online marketplace that PChome Online launched in 2000. Since 2007, it has offered a delivery service promising "year-round, 24-hour delivery." PChome Online's official website describes PChome 24h as Taiwan's largest B2C site (as of November 2026).

The PChome Online group also includes Ruten, a C2C (consumer-to-consumer) marketplace; Bibian, a proxy shopping service for products from Japan and the United States; and PChome Express, its logistics company. Bibian is a service that helps Taiwanese consumers buy Japanese products. It is not a marketplace where Japanese businesses open stores.

On how to get listed, PChome Online's corporate website has no page for merchant recruitment or seller requirements for overseas businesses. The 2017 JETRO report stated that PChome required a local corporate registration in Taiwan, but also noted that some channels allowed non-Taiwanese entities to list products, with conditions varying by category and product, and recommended confirming directly. Since the rules may have changed, the safest approach is to contact PChome directly or consult a Taiwanese importer.

### Shopee: A Marketplace You Can Join Directly from Japan

Shopee is a marketplace founded in Singapore in 2015. For cross-border sales from Japan, it covers seven markets: Taiwan, Singapore, Thailand, Malaysia, the Philippines, Vietnam, and Brazil. Its official website describes Shopee as "the largest ecommerce platform in Southeast Asia and Taiwan" (as of November 2026).

According to Shopee Japan's official website, the main requirements for selling from Japan are as follows (as of November 2026):

- No setup or monthly fees
- Both companies and individuals can open a store
- List at least five products within 30 days of applying for an account
- Ship directly from Japan to buyers, not from a local warehouse
- Ship within two business days of receiving an order (three business days for Taiwan, excluding Japanese weekends and public holidays)
- Buyer support is generally in English, and in Traditional Chinese for Taiwan
- Sales proceeds are paid out weekly to a Payoneer (international payments service) account

Opening a store takes four steps:

1. Fill out the account application form on Shopee Japan's website
2. Follow the instructions in the invitation email you receive
3. Set up your shop's basic settings in Seller Centre (the seller dashboard)
4. List your products to complete the setup

New sellers can choose Singapore, Taiwan, Malaysia, or the Philippines as their first market, so you can start with Taiwan right away. For shipping, you choose from carriers such as Japan Post, DHL, FedEx, Sagawa Express, Yamato Transport, and ECMS based on the product and weight, and Shopee covers part of the international shipping cost (conditions apply). Shopee Japan's seller guide pages do not list sales fee rates, so check the seller information before you apply. For more on Shopee's big sales events, see "[Shopee's "11.11 Mega Day": Japanese Sellers' GMV Jumped 24x](https://www.leap.site/en/ec-guide/cross-border/shopee-1111-mega-day-cross-border-ec/)."

### Other Marketplaces Such as Yahoo Kimo Shopping Center

Besides the three marketplaces above, Yahoo Kimo Shopping Center is another well-known marketplace in Taiwan. Its website has a "合作招商" (merchant partnership) link and a "企業/大宗採購入口" (corporate and bulk purchasing) entry point. The 2017 JETRO report stated that, like momo and PChome, it required a local corporate registration in Taiwan.

## Three Ways to Sell on Taiwanese Marketplaces from Japan

There are three main ways for a Japanese business to sell on Taiwanese marketplaces:

1. **Sell directly from Japan (Shopee, etc.)**: You keep your Japanese company and ship directly to buyers from Japan. It is easy to start, but you handle buyer support in Traditional Chinese and international shipping yourself.
2. **List through a Taiwanese importer or wholesale partner (momo, PChome, etc.)**: Your Taiwanese partner imports the products and handles the relationship with the marketplace and sales. This reduces your workload, but it is harder to control pricing and presentation.
3. **Set up a Taiwanese entity and deal with marketplaces directly**: You work with marketplaces as a local company. This gives you the most control, but setting up and running an entity costs money, so it makes sense once you have a clear sales outlook in Taiwan.

Whichever route you take, product information shown to Taiwanese shoppers needs to be in Traditional Chinese. The 2017 JETRO report noted that product packaging can stay in Japanese, but labels and advertising must be in Traditional Chinese, and it advised against simply converting Simplified Chinese translations into Traditional Chinese because of differences in phrasing. For regulated products such as cosmetics and food, always check the latest rules from Taiwanese authorities in advance.

## How to Choose a Taiwan Marketplace

Which channel to choose for selling to Taiwan depends on your resources and goals. As a rule of thumb:

- **You want to test demand in Taiwan on a small scale while staying a Japanese company**: Shopee
- **You already have a Taiwanese importer or partner, or a Taiwanese entity**: momo and PChome 24h
- **You are not yet known in Taiwan and want to build a sales track record quickly using a marketplace's traffic**: Shopee
- **You want AI to handle overseas marketing such as SEO, email newsletters, and localization with minimal staff, and grow customers on your own site**: Leap

You can also combine channels: test demand on a marketplace while building your brand and customer data on your own store. ## FAQ

**Q. What are the most popular ecommerce sites in Taiwan?**

A. The most representative ecommerce sites in Taiwan are momo, PChome 24h, and Shopee. momo and PChome are major marketplaces that each describe themselves on their official websites as Taiwan's largest B2C site, and Shopee is a marketplace where many independent stores sell. Yahoo Kimo Shopping Center is also well known.

**Q. Can I sell on momo directly from Japan?**

A. Whether you can sell on momo directly from Japan cannot be confirmed from its official pages as of November 2026. The 2017 JETRO report stated that momo required a local corporate registration in Taiwan. Japanese businesses typically sell through a Taiwanese importer or wholesale partner.

**Q. How can a Japanese company start selling on a Taiwanese marketplace?**

A. The easiest way for a Japanese company is to join Shopee's Taiwan market through Shopee Japan. You apply via the online form, follow the instructions in the invitation email, and complete setup by listing at least five products within 30 days. If you want to sell on momo or PChome, consult a Taiwanese importer, or set up a Taiwanese entity and then contact each marketplace.

**Q. Does it cost anything to sell on Shopee Taiwan?**

A. Shopee's cross-border program from Japan has no setup or monthly fees (as of November 2026). However, sellers arrange and pay for international shipping, and sales fee rates are not listed on Shopee Japan's seller guide pages, so check the seller information before applying.

## Summary and Key Takeaway

When selling to Taiwan, what matters more than a marketplace's size or brand is how much you can do on your own as a Japanese company. momo and PChome are major players in Taiwan, but they do not publish requirements for overseas sellers, so selling there generally means going through a Taiwanese partner or entity. Shopee lets you join directly from Japan with no setup or monthly fees, which makes it a good first step for testing demand in Taiwan.

In other words, choosing a Taiwan marketplace is not about which one is biggest. It is about who handles the work on the Taiwan side. If you will do it yourself, choose Shopee. If you will rely on a Taiwanese partner, choose momo or PChome. If you want to own the customer relationship, choose your own online store. Framing it this way makes the right channel for your business much clearer.
