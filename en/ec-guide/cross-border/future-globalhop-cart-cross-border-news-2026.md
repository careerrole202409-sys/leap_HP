## Quick Overview: future Globalhop Lets You List on Major Marketplaces in Taiwan and Singapore in One Go

future Globalhop is a cross-border selling service that futureshop Inc., the company behind the Japanese ecommerce platform "futureshop," launched on November 2, 2026. Sellers can list products on Taiwan's "momo購物網 (momo shop)" and on Shopee in Taiwan and Singapore through "futura select," a Japanese-brand store run by futureshop Inc. Translation, logistics, customs, local customer support, and payouts are all handled for you. You don't need to be a futureshop customer to apply.

In this article, we walk through future Globalhop's marketplaces, pricing, and caveats based on official sources, and calculate where each pricing plan starts to pay off. We also explain how it differs from the cross-border options offered by other Japanese ecommerce platforms such as BASE, STORES, and makeshop byGMO, and cover the GMO Pepabo and GMO MakeShop integration talks announced in November 2026.

## What Is future Globalhop?

future Globalhop is a listing support service that lets Japanese ecommerce businesses sell on major marketplaces in Taiwan and Singapore without setting up a local entity or signing separate contracts with each marketplace. futureshop Inc. announced the service on October 15, 2026, and launched it on November 2, 2026.

Here's how it works: futureshop Inc. operates a store called "futura select" on momo and Shopee, and your products are sold through that store. In other words, you don't open your own marketplace account; you sell via futura select. According to the official website, any business that already sells through its own online store can apply, whether or not it uses futureshop.

## Marketplaces and Services Covered

As of November 2026, future Globalhop covers the three storefronts below. A single plan lets you list on all three, and you can also choose which marketplaces and countries to sell in.

| Marketplace | Country/Region |
|---|---|
| momo購物網 (momo shop) | Taiwan |
| Shopee | Taiwan |
| Shopee | Singapore |

According to futureshop Inc.'s announcement, momo draws about 40 million visitors a month and is especially popular with women in their 30s to 50s. Shopee is described as strong during major sales events such as Double 11 (November 11) and Double 12 (December 12). The company plans to expand to Lazada, Tmall, and major marketplaces in China, the US, and Europe, but no timeline has been given.

According to the official website, the service includes:

- Store setup and listing (as futura select)
- Product page translation and creation (register an existing product page URL and it's translated with AI)
- Logistics (from labeling through customs clearance)
- Handling inquiries from local customers
- Payouts in Japanese yen
- Accepting returns
- Joining marketplace campaigns and advice on promotions

Getting started takes five steps: 1. apply (sign an agreement), 2. choose products (decide which items to list, pricing, and sales terms), 3. connect product data (via CSV or product page URLs), 4. translation and page creation, and 5. operation and launch.

## future Globalhop Pricing (as of November 2026)

future Globalhop offers three plans, each combining a 20,000 yen setup fee with a monthly fee and a sales commission (all prices exclude tax).

| Plan | Setup fee | Monthly fee | Sales commission | Max listings | Add-ons |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Free | 20,000 yen | 0 yen | 15% | 30 products | Not available |
| Standard | 20,000 yen | 30,000 yen | 10% | momo 200 / Shopee 50 | Available |
| Premium | 20,000 yen | 100,000 yen | 5% | momo 500 / Shopee 100 | Available |

Keep in mind that each marketplace also charges its own fees on top of the sales commission, including selling fees, payment fees, and currency conversion fees. Pricing for add-ons such as advertising and promotion is available on request. According to the press release, the Standard and Premium plans include ongoing support for localization, sales analysis, and social media and ad management.

### Our Calculation: Which Plan Saves You the Most?

We used the published pricing to find the break-even points between plans. Since the setup fee is the same 20,000 yen for every plan, we compared only the monthly fee and sales commission (marketplace fees and consumption tax are excluded).

| Monthly sales | Free (15%) | Standard (30,000 yen + 10%) | Premium (100,000 yen + 5%) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 300,000 yen | 45,000 yen | 60,000 yen | 115,000 yen |
| 600,000 yen | 90,000 yen | 90,000 yen | 130,000 yen |
| 1,000,000 yen | 150,000 yen | 130,000 yen | 150,000 yen |
| 1,400,000 yen | 210,000 yen | 170,000 yen | 170,000 yen |
| 2,000,000 yen | 300,000 yen | 230,000 yen | 200,000 yen |

Once monthly sales pass 600,000 yen, Standard becomes cheaper than Free, and once they pass 1,400,000 yen, Premium becomes cheaper than Standard. That said, the Free plan caps listings at 30 products, so sellers with larger catalogs will need Standard or higher regardless of expected sales. A practical approach is to test your best-sellers on the Free plan first and switch to Standard as monthly sales approach 600,000 yen.

## Things to Check Before Using future Globalhop

Before signing up, make sure you understand five conditions: inventory, shipping, duties, currency, and returns. The official FAQ explains them as follows.

1. **Keep separate inventory**: As of November 2026, automatic inventory sync isn't supported, so you need to set aside stock specifically for future Globalhop. System integration with futureshop is planned.
2. **Ship within two business days of an order**: Marketplace rules require shipment within two business days.
3. **Duties are paid by the buyer**: In Taiwan, buyers handle customs duties through an app called "EZWAY." Only items that have cleared customs are counted as confirmed orders.
4. **Currency is converted once a month**: momo uses the rate at the time of purchase, and Shopee uses the Monday rate when sales are confirmed, both converting to US dollars. Conversion from US dollars to yen happens once a month at the time of payout.
5. **Return windows apply**: Under Taiwanese law, buyers can return items within seven days of delivery. Marketplace rules set the window at 10 days for momo and 7 days for Shopee.

Some products can't be sold due to laws or marketplace rules, including pharmaceuticals, animals, and certain supplements. Apparel, cosmetics, and household goods are listed as categories that are easy to sell. Because you set the local price yourself, including international shipping, it doesn't have to match your price in Japan.

## How It Compares With Other Platforms' Cross-Border Options

Japanese ecommerce platforms have been rolling out cross-border selling options one after another in 2026. They all share the same basic idea of "ship to a domestic warehouse and sell overseas," but they differ in where you sell and how fees work.

| Service | Launch | Where you sell | Main costs for sellers | Who can use it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| future Globalhop (futureshop) | November 2, 2026 | Marketplaces in Taiwan and Singapore (momo, Shopee) | 20,000 yen setup fee + plan-based monthly fee and commission, plus marketplace fees | Open to non-futureshop users |
| Kantan Kaigai Hanbai / Easy Overseas Sales (BASE) | Became a standard feature on March 18, 2026 | Your own BASE store (reach to 100+ countries) | 5% of the payment amount, only when you sell overseas (plan fees separate) | BASE stores |
| Kaigai Hanbai Renkei / Overseas Sales Integration (STORES) | July 7, 2026 | Your own STORES online store (228 countries and regions) | No setup or monthly fees (STORES usage fees separate). Buyers pay a 10% proxy fee on the product price plus domestic shipping | STORES online store users |
| Japan Finds byGMO (GMO Global EC) | February 9, 2026 | Overseas marketplaces (starting with eBay and others at announcement) | Free, including setup, monthly, sales, and international shipping fees (makeshop byGMO fees separate) | makeshop byGMO users |

(Compiled from each company's press releases and official websites as of November 2026. Japan Finds byGMO reflects its February 2026 announcement.)

BASE's Easy Overseas Sales and STORES' Overseas Sales Integration bring overseas buyers into your own online store and deliver orders abroad through a proxy purchasing model. future Globalhop and Japan Finds byGMO, on the other hand, put your products on overseas marketplaces so you can sell to the shoppers those marketplaces already attract. If your store already gets overseas traffic, the former tends to fit better; if you want to tap into local marketplaces' reach, the latter is usually the better match.

## Another Development: GMO Pepabo and GMO MakeShop Integration Talks

The ecommerce platform space saw another major announcement on November 13, 2026. GMO Pepabo, which runs "Color Me Shop byGMOペパボ," announced that it will establish a holding company, "GMO AI Commerce Holdings, Inc." (tentative name), and move to a holding company structure on April 1, 2027.

On the same day, the company decided to begin talks to make GMO MakeShop, which runs "makeshop byGMO" and "GMO Cloud EC," a wholly owned subsidiary of the holding company. The integration is expected to take the form of a share exchange, but the terms and timing are undecided, and it may not go ahead depending on the outcome of the talks. The announcement lists the expected synergies as faster AI-driven product and feature development, shared sales, marketing, and system infrastructure, and closer ties with adjacent areas such as logistics.

For sellers using Color Me Shop or makeshop byGMO, nothing changes immediately as of November 2026. The company says GMO Pepabo will continue to run its existing businesses under the holding company. Still, if the integration moves forward, features and the scope of cross-border support could change, so it's worth keeping an eye on official announcements.

## Marketplace Listings vs. Your Own Online Store

Marketplace listing services like future Globalhop let you test the waters on a small scale by borrowing local marketplaces' traffic. On the other hand, because you sell through futura select, a store run by another company, they're less suited to owning customer data and building repeat buyers.

One approach is to start with marketplace listings to see how each country responds, then grow your brand through your own cross-border online store in the countries that show promise. ## FAQ

**Q. Can I use future Globalhop if I don't use futureshop?**

A. Yes. The official website says any business that sells through its own online store can apply, not just futureshop users.

**Q. How much does future Globalhop cost?**

A. There are three plans, each with a 20,000 yen setup fee plus a monthly fee and sales commission (as of November 2026, excluding tax). Free is 0 yen per month with a 15% commission, Standard is 30,000 yen per month with 10%, and Premium is 100,000 yen per month with 5%. Marketplace fees are charged separately.

**Q. Which countries can I sell to with future Globalhop?**

A. As of November 2026, Taiwan and Singapore. You can list on three storefronts: momo shop and Shopee in Taiwan, and Shopee in Singapore. Expansion to Lazada, Tmall, and marketplaces in China, the US, and Europe is planned, but no timeline has been given.

**Q. Who pays customs duties on sales to Taiwan?**

A. The buyer does. According to the official website, buyers in Taiwan handle customs duties through an app called "EZWAY," and only items that have cleared customs are counted as confirmed orders.
