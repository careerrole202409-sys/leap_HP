## Quick Overview: Cross-Border Operation Agencies Split Into "Marketplace Specialists" and "All-in-One Support"

When you want to outsource running your cross-border EC operation, agencies broadly fall into two types: ones that specialize in a single marketplace like Amazon or Tmall Global, and ones that bundle marketing with logistics support across the board. Most don't publish pricing — a quote is the norm. This guide looks at what real operation agencies actually cover, and compares outsourcing against handling it with AI instead.

## What a Cross-Border Operation Agency Actually Covers

A cross-border operation agency handles everything from setting up a store on an overseas marketplace, building and localizing product pages, customer support in the local language, promotion, and arranging logistics — all outsourced to one company. It's typically used by sellers who lack cross-border expertise in-house, or staff who can handle the local language.

How much falls under "handled for you" varies a lot between providers — some specialize in running a single marketplace, while others work across marketplaces and shopping carts broadly. Before reaching out, it's worth deciding how much you actually want outsourced: just the marketplace setup, or day-to-day customer support and promotion too.

## A Marketplace Specialist: ZonExpert (Amazon Overseas Expansion)

Per ZonExpert's official site, its "Amazon Overseas Sales Expansion" service bundles everything needed to sell on the US Amazon marketplace.

- Covers trade document preparation, regulatory compliance, customs handling, and delivery to Amazon's own overseas FBA warehouses
- The only overseas market explicitly named is the US, described as roughly 15 times the size of Japan's Amazon market
- Existing domestic Amazon operation clients can switch plans to add overseas listing support
- Pricing for the overseas service isn't listed on the site — you need to call or use the contact form

The defining feature here is specializing in a single marketplace, Amazon, while taking on hands-on work like customs handling and FBA delivery.

## A China Specialist: fcafe (Tmall Global)

fcafe Inc. runs store operations on Tmall Global, one of China's largest marketplaces. Per media coverage of its partnership with logistics company 4PX Express Japan, fcafe's operation service covers:

- Drafting a business plan and handling the Tmall Global store application process
- Product listing and storefront setup, including building Simplified Chinese pages
- Customer support in Simplified Chinese
- Promotion both on and off the marketplace
- Consulting on cross-border EC operations

The company has reportedly run cross-border EC operations for China since 2008, with a track record (as of the partnership announcement) of supporting over 3,000 brands and ¥12 billion in cumulative transaction value. Pricing isn't published.

## All-in-One Marketing and Logistics: Sagawa's "Overseas EC Full Support"

As an example that isn't tied to one marketplace, Sagawa Express launched its "Overseas EC Full Support" service on August 1, 2023. Per industry media coverage, it bundles:

- Marketing support for getting listed, including market research and promotion proposals, plus recommendations on which countries and marketplaces to target
- A dedicated system that connects via API to multiple overseas marketplaces and carts, managing everything from order intake through shipping in one place
- Support for warehouse storage and management through international shipping
- Access to a network of partner companies experienced in overseas sales and marketing

It's aimed at both sellers just starting overseas and sellers already running cross-border EC who are struggling with the complexity of managing inventory and shipping across multiple marketplaces. Pricing and the specific countries or marketplaces covered aren't detailed in the coverage.

## How Operation Agency Pricing Gets Decided

None of the three agencies covered here publish specific pricing for their overseas service on their website — a quote is the starting point in all three cases. Pricing likely depends on the scope requested, the number of marketplaces involved, and whether local-language support is needed, so it's worth describing your scope concretely when requesting a quote.

## FAQ

**Q. What kinds of cross-border EC operation agencies are there?**

A. There are marketplace specialists (like ZonExpert for Amazon), country/marketplace specialists (like fcafe for China's Tmall Global), and broader agencies that bundle marketing and logistics across multiple marketplaces (like Sagawa's Overseas EC Full Support).

**Q. How much does an operation agency cost?**

A. None of the agencies covered here publish specific pricing for their overseas service — it depends on the scope of work and the number of marketplaces involved, so you need to request a quote.

**Q. Is it better to list on a marketplace and use an agency, or build my own EC site?**

A. Listing on a marketplace lets you tap into existing traffic right away; building your own site is the route if you want to grow a long-term brand following. If you want AI to handle everything from building your own site through overseas marketing, a tool like Leap is worth considering.

**Q. What should I check when choosing an operation agency?**

A. The marketplace or country it covers, the scope of work it handles (just setup, or day-to-day operations and customer support too), and how pricing is structured (flat fee vs. performance-based, for example).
