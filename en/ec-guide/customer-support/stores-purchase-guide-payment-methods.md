## Quick Overview: A Purchase Guide That Cuts Down on Seller Support Load

People searching "how to buy on STORES" fall into two groups: shoppers about to buy something, and sellers who want to explain the process to their customers. This guide covers the basic steps from adding an item to your cart through completing checkout, the difference between creating a STORES account and checking out as a guest, the main payment methods available, and what to do if your order confirmation email never shows up. Sellers are welcome to link straight to this guide from their own FAQ page.

## The Basic Steps From Cart to Checkout

Buying on STORES follows a flow similar to most online shops. You add the item you want to your cart, review what's in it, enter your shipping details and payment method, confirm your order, and complete the purchase. Once it's done, a confirmation email is typically sent to the address you provided. The exact screens and button labels can vary by shop design and change as the system updates, so when in doubt, just follow the instructions on screen for the shop you're buying from.

## Creating a STORES Account vs. Checking Out as a Guest

Many online shops support both creating an account before you buy and checking out as a guest, without one. Creating an account means you won't have to re-enter your shipping details next time, and you can view your past orders all in one place. Guest checkout is a convenient option if you just want to buy once without the extra step. We weren't able to fully confirm the specifics of how STORES's guest checkout works, or exactly how it differs from a registered account, from official sources at the time of writing. Whether a particular shop supports guest checkout or requires an account can depend on that shop's own settings, so if you're unsure, check the checkout screen's guidance or ask the shop directly.

## What to Do If Your Confirmation Email Never Arrives

Sometimes a confirmation email takes a while to show up after you complete a purchase, or never arrives at all. Common causes include a mistyped email address at checkout, or the message landing in a spam folder. If you can't find it, check your spam folder first, and if it's still not there, double-check that you entered your email address correctly when you ordered. If that doesn't resolve it, reaching out directly to the shop you bought from is the next step — they can look up your order details for you. We weren't able to fully confirm the specifics of how STORES handles resending a confirmation email from official sources at the time of writing. Whether that feature exists is worth checking with the shop you bought from, or STORES's own help center.

## FAQ

**Q. Do I need to create an account to buy on STORES?**

A. Depending on the shop, you may be able to check out without creating an account. The exact setup can vary by shop, so check the guidance on the checkout screen to see whether an account is required.

**Q. Can I use the same payment methods on every shop?**

A. No. STORES Payments supports a wide range of methods — credit cards, e-money, and QR code payments among them — but which ones you can actually use depends on what the specific shop has set up. Check what's available at checkout for the shop you're buying from.

**Q. What's the first thing to do if my confirmation email doesn't arrive?**

A. Check your spam folder first, and if it's not there, confirm that you entered your email address correctly when you ordered. If that doesn't resolve it, the next step is contacting the shop you bought from directly.
