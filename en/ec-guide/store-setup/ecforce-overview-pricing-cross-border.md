## Quick Overview: What Is ecforce? Pricing, Features, and Cross-Border Support

ecforce is a hosted ("ASP") ecommerce cart platform run by Japan's SUPER STUDIO, built so merchants don't need to run their own servers. Pricing is built around two main plans — Standard and Expert — plus per-order usage fees. Cross-border selling isn't a built-in feature; it's only available through a separate integration with a service called Buyee Connect. This guide checks the pricing breakdown and the real state of cross-border support against official sources, and gives an honest read on who ecforce actually fits.

## What ecforce Actually Does

ecforce is designed to cover everything from launching a store to running and growing it, in one system. Its core features include:

- **Cart functionality**: product management, order management, template-based design, and custom builds
- **Subscription and bundle sales**: built-in support for recurring/subscription sales and bundled products
- **LPO/EFO tools**: features aimed at improving landing-page conversion and checkout form completion rates
- **CRM tools**: sales aggregation, customer management, and subscription retention analytics
- **Personalization**: centralized management of each customer's purchase history and characteristics
- **Ad management and analytics**: performance tracking by ad URL and A/B testing

ecforce doesn't include payment processing itself — credit cards, deferred payment (NP Atobarai, Paidy, etc.), e-money (PayPay, Amazon Pay, Rakuten Pay), cash on delivery, and bank transfer all require a separate contract with a payment processor. Given how much weight it puts on subscriptions, LPO, and CRM, ecforce is built with repeat-purchase D2C brands in mind more than one-off sales.

## ecforce Pricing (as of November 2026)

According to ecforce's official pricing page, pricing is built around two main plans, plus a dedicated migration plan for merchants switching from another cart:

| Plan | Setup fee | Monthly fee | Order volume cap |
|---|---|---|---|
| Standard | ¥148,000 | ¥49,800 | Up to 2,000 orders/month |
| Expert | ¥248,000 | ¥99,800 | No cap |
| Migration (switching from another cart) | Custom quote | ¥99,800 | - |

On top of the monthly fee, there's a per-order usage charge of ¥30. Payment processing fees come on top of that, set by whichever payment processor you contract with separately. Since sources vary on whether figures are tax-inclusive, it's worth confirming the current numbers directly with ecforce before signing up.

## Cross-Border Support: Only Through the Buyee Connect Integration

ecforce's own standard feature set doesn't include dedicated cross-border ecommerce functionality. In January 2023, SUPER STUDIO (ecforce's operator) began integrating with Buyee Connect, the cross-border support service run by tenso, the company behind the overseas shopping proxy service Buyee.

Through this integration, merchants using ecforce can add a dedicated overseas checkout to their existing site just by inserting a tag — no separate rebuild required. The overseas cart can display in one of 10 languages, with checkout handled on Buyee's own system, supporting payment methods commonly used overseas like PayPal and UnionPay. Buyee also handles overseas shipping and multilingual customer support, meaning the Japanese merchant can keep running their domestic operations largely as-is while selling overseas. The integration is reported to reach 118 countries and regions, with no setup or monthly fee for the integration itself.

It's worth being clear that this is an external integration, not a built-in ecforce feature. Signing up goes through BeeCruise, the company that handles Buyee Connect inquiries.

## Who ecforce Actually Fits

Based on the above, ecforce tends to suit:

- D2C brands built around subscriptions or bundled products
- Merchants who want strong post-sale tools — CRM, retention analytics, LPO
- Businesses comfortable with the setup and monthly cost in exchange for no order-volume cap (the Expert plan)

It's worth looking elsewhere if:

- You want to start small domestically first — the ¥148,000+ setup fee is a bigger commitment than free plans from BASE or STORES
- You want cross-border marketing (multilingual SEO, overseas email, etc.) bundled in — ecforce itself doesn't offer that, so you'd need to combine it with another service

## FAQ

**Q. How much does ecforce cost?**

A. The Standard plan is ¥148,000 to set up plus ¥49,800/month (up to 2,000 orders/month), and the Expert plan is ¥248,000 to set up plus ¥99,800/month (no order cap). Both carry a ¥30 per-order usage fee plus separate payment processing fees (as of November 2026).

**Q. Can I migrate to ecforce from another cart?**

A. Yes — there's a dedicated migration plan for merchants switching carts. Setup is a custom quote, with a ¥99,800 monthly fee.

**Q. Does ecforce support cross-border ecommerce?**

A. Not as a built-in feature. It reaches overseas buyers through a separate integration with Buyee Connect, which adds an overseas checkout to your site via a single tag.

**Q. How is ecforce different from Shopify?**

A. ecforce is a Japan-focused hosted cart meant to be paired with domestic payment processors and logistics providers, with particular strength in subscriptions and CRM. Shopify ships with multilingual and multi-currency features and a wide range of overseas-facing apps out of the box, so it tends to have more built-in depth if cross-border selling itself is the main goal.

## Summary and Key Takeaway

ecforce is a Japan-focused hosted cart with real strength in what happens after the sale — subscriptions, CRM, and LPO. Its pricing is clear plan-by-plan, but total cost shifts with order volume and whichever payment processor you pick, so it's worth checking the usage fee and processing rates together, not just the plan price. On cross-border, it's worth understanding upfront that ecforce itself doesn't include it — Buyee Connect is a separate integration — so you can decide how much to handle in-house versus hand off to an external service.
