# Shopify vs. Japan's Major E-Commerce Platforms: BASE, STORES & ColorMe Compared

## [Quick Summary] Shopify, BASE, STORES, or ColorMe — Which Should You Choose?

Anyone considering building an online store inevitably faces the same question: which platform should I use? Shopify is often compared to BASE, STORES, and ColorMe Shop, but each platform is designed for a completely different type of user.

To cut to the chase: if you want to start small with zero upfront or monthly costs — as an individual seller or side business — BASE or STORES are the natural choices. If you're aiming to build a mid-sized domestic store with reliable support, ColorMe Shop fits the bill. But if you're serious about scaling, cross-border e-commerce, or multi-channel operations, Shopify stands apart with unmatched extensibility.

This article breaks down the differences in pricing, features, scalability, and cross-border capabilities across all four platforms, so EC managers and business owners can make a well-informed decision.

## Understanding Where Each Platform Fits

Before diving into a feature-by-feature comparison, it's important to understand who each platform is actually built for. Skipping this step and comparing on price alone often leads to regret down the line — "the features weren't enough" or "the expansion costs were way higher than expected."

BASE is designed for individuals and side-business owners who want to launch an online shop as quickly, cheaply, and easily as possible. Optimized for smartphone use, it requires zero web or programming knowledge and lets you start selling on the same day.

STORES occupies a similar space to BASE, but is particularly well-suited for small brick-and-mortar businesses that want to manage their physical and online stores in one place. It's designed to work alongside POS registers and booking systems, making it popular among owners who prioritize simplicity.

ColorMe Shop, operated by GMO Pepabo, is a Japan-made EC platform used by over 50,000 merchants nationwide (as of January 2025). Packed with features tailored to Japanese business practices and backed by phone and email support, it's a strong fit for domestic businesses aiming to build a mid-sized online store with hands-on guidance.

Shopify is a global commerce platform from Canada, deployed by over 1.7 million stores across more than 175 countries. Its greatest strength is the ability to manage domestic e-commerce, cross-border sales, B2B, and multi-channel operations all in one place — making it the go-to choice for businesses that want to scale and grow without switching platforms.

## Comparing Initial Costs, Monthly Fees, and Transaction Fees

The cost structure varies dramatically between platforms. Understanding the balance between fixed and variable costs (transaction fees) relative to your sales volume is essential to making the right choice.

BASE has no setup fees or monthly charges. However, on the Standard plan, a payment processing fee (3.6% + ¥40) and a service fee (3%) apply separately. While costs stay low at small sales volumes, the variable fee burden increases as monthly revenue grows. The Growth plan at ¥16,580/month reduces those fees, so switching becomes worthwhile once you hit a certain sales threshold.

STORES also offers a free plan with no monthly fee, but its payment processing fee is a relatively high 5.5%. The Standard plan is ¥3,630/month and brings the rate down to 3.6%. The platform's simplicity comes at the cost of design and feature flexibility, and many sellers find it limiting as their business grows.

ColorMe Shop's free plan has no monthly fee, setup costs, or sales commission — but it comes with a 200MB disk limit and a cap on the number of images. For practical ongoing use, the Regular plan (¥3,300 setup fee + ¥4,950/month) is the realistic baseline. Paid plans charge zero sales commission, and credit card fees start from 3.4%, making it notably cost-effective over time.

Shopify is unique among the four in that it requires a monthly base fee. The Basic plan is ¥4,850/month (or ¥3,650/month on annual billing), the Grow plan is ¥13,500/month (¥10,100/month annual), and the Advanced plan is ¥58,500/month (¥44,000/month annual). All plans include a 3-day free trial, and a promotional rate of ¥150/month applies for the first three months. With Shopify Payments, there are no additional fees for external payment methods — meaning the more your sales grow, the more cost-efficient Shopify becomes.

In summary: BASE and STORES work well for small businesses that want to minimize fixed costs. ColorMe Shop and Shopify make sense for those focused on long-term operational cost efficiency. And for businesses aiming for serious scale, Shopify is the most rational choice.

## Extensibility and Cross-Border E-Commerce Capabilities

Equally important to pricing is the question of whether the platform can support what you want to do in the future. The difference becomes especially significant when you're planning business growth, overseas expansion, or multi-channel operations.

BASE and STORES offer limited customization — primarily template-based with restricted or no direct HTML/CSS editing. The features available are confined to the apps and plugins each platform provides, making it difficult to handle advanced CRM campaigns, multi-channel integrations, or centralized inventory management. For cross-border e-commerce, BASE does support overseas sales and multi-currency display, but content not in English requires manual input. STORES has no foreign currency display support at all.

ColorMe Shop allows HTML/CSS editing and offers a range of extensions for domestic merchants, including WordPress integration. However, multilingual support is not included as standard — a paid app is required — so full cross-border operations can be constrained.

Shopify's extensibility is in a league of its own. Thousands of apps cover SEO, advertising, social media, CRM, inventory, and logistics — virtually every e-commerce function can be added on demand. Over 14 free and 227+ paid themes are available, all responsive by design. Using Shopify's native templating language, Liquid, enables deep customization far beyond what themes offer out of the box.

On cross-border capability, Shopify natively supports 130+ currencies and 50+ languages, with tax handling, shipping settings, and payment methods by country all manageable from a single admin dashboard. On the Plus plan, separate country-specific storefronts can be created and managed under one brand — a setup built from the ground up for global expansion.

What all these cases share is that Shopify's extensibility proves effective when businesses look beyond domestic sales to international expansion, brand building, and data-driven strategy.

## Choosing the Right Platform by Business Stage and Goals

Here's a clear breakdown of which platform to choose based on your current situation and future goals.

**Starting small as a side business, individual seller, or test run**: BASE is the clear choice. Zero setup and monthly fees mean you can start selling today using just your smartphone. For those wanting to validate their e-commerce idea with minimal risk, it's the optimal starting point.

**Small business with a physical store, wanting to manage online and offline in one place**: STORES is the right fit. Its seamless integration with POS registers and booking systems makes unified management straightforward.

**Targeting monthly sales in the millions while running a solid domestic operation with reliable support**: ColorMe Shop is a strong candidate. Its phone and email support makes it manageable even for teams new to e-commerce, and the platform is optimized for Japanese payment methods, shipping, and business practices.

**Mid-to-large businesses targeting hundreds of millions in annual revenue, with cross-border sales, multi-channel expansion, and data utilization in mind**: Shopify is the right choice. The ability to scale plans as you grow, combined with native multilingual and multi-currency support, a vast app ecosystem, and a proven global track record, sets it clearly apart.

One important factor to consider: the cost of migrating platforms later. Many sellers start on BASE or STORES, see their sales grow, and then move to Shopify — but transferring product data, customer records, and order history takes significant time and money. If you're already targeting monthly sales in the tens of millions, building on Shopify from the start is likely to cost less overall in the long run.

## FAQ

**Q1. I've heard Shopify and BASE cost about the same monthly — is that true?**
Looking at fixed monthly fees alone, they may appear similar in some scenarios. But total cost of ownership, including variable fees (transaction fees and service charges), varies significantly depending on sales volume. BASE's Growth plan at ¥16,580/month does reduce fees, but comes at a high fixed cost. With Shopify Payments, there are no additional transaction fees for external payment methods — so as sales grow, Shopify becomes increasingly cost-efficient. If your monthly sales exceed several million yen, we strongly recommend comparing total costs rather than just the monthly base fee.

**Q2. If I want to start domestically but eventually expand overseas, should I start with Shopify from the beginning?**
If overseas expansion is even a possibility, we recommend starting with Shopify from day one. Migrating later from BASE or ColorMe Shop to a cross-border-capable platform involves product data migration, customer data transfer, SEO continuity concerns, and new build costs — unexpected effort and expense. With Shopify, you can build for the domestic market now while quietly setting up the multilingual and multi-currency configurations you'll need later — ensuring a smooth transition when the time comes.

**Q3. Shopify seems overwhelming with so many features. Can beginners actually manage it?**
Shopify's admin dashboard is designed to be intuitive, and basic operations — product listings, order management, payment setup — don't require any coding knowledge. That said, advanced SEO work, app integrations, and deeper customization do involve a learning curve, and some teams find it useful to bring in outside expertise. Shopify has a large network of certified partners (Shopify Experts) who can support build, operations, and marketing at every stage. A practical approach is to start simple on a basic plan and add capabilities as your business grows.

## Conclusion: Choose Your E-Commerce Platform by Working Backward from Your Growth Strategy

Shopify, BASE, STORES, and ColorMe Shop are each built with a distinct user in mind. The question isn't which platform is "better" — it's which one is right for where your business is now and where it's going.

For launching lean with minimal cost: BASE or STORES. For stable domestic operations with solid support: ColorMe Shop. For serious scaling and global ambitions: Shopify.

For EC managers and business owners targeting hundreds of millions in annual revenue, platform selection isn't just a tool choice — it's a foundational infrastructure decision that affects future migration costs, cross-border viability, and the range of marketing strategies available to you.
