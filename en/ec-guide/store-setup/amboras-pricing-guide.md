## Amboras Pricing at a Glance

Amboras is an AI-native e-commerce platform selected for Y Combinator's Spring 2026 batch. It handles store design generation, A/B testing, and automated email campaigns through AI — covering nearly the entire operational stack without external app integrations. That combination has generated significant interest among e-commerce operators.

This article covers the pricing structure in full: the monthly fees and feature differences across the four plans (Basic, Pro, Advanced, and Plus as of November 2026), how the 30-day launch guarantee works, transaction fee structure, and a side-by-side cost comparison with Shopify.

The short answer: you can start building a store on Amboras for free with no credit card, but you need a paid plan to publish on your own domain and sell in earnest. As of November 2026, the pricing page lists four paid plans: Basic ($49/month), Pro ($105/month), Advanced ($299/month), and Plus (contact sales), all billed monthly. What sets it apart is that AI automation tools — image enhancement, email automation, A/B testing, and more — are all built in at every tier. When those tools are factored against the cost of equivalent Shopify apps, Amboras' all-in pricing often compares favorably.

## The Four Amboras Plans

Amboras pricing is structured in four tiers, designed to scale with business size. Both monthly and annual billing (20% off) are available. The prices below reflect monthly billing as of November 2026.

**Basic: $49/month (as of November 2026)**

Described on the Amboras pricing page as the plan "for starting and running your first store." Suited to individual merchants and early-stage startups. Includes the AI Store Designer, AI image generation and enhancement, and email campaigns and flows, for 1 store, 1 seat, and 1 custom domain (as of November 2026). Multi-currency and automatic tax, A/B/n testing, and priority support are not included.

**Pro: $105/month (as of November 2026)**

The plan officially labeled "Most Popular," designed for ambitious brands ready to grow. Everything in Basic, plus up to 5 stores, 10 seats, and 5 custom domains, AI A/B/n testing, multi-currency and automatic tax, and priority support (as of November 2026). Advanced analytics is not included. The standout feature for cross-border merchants: 5 stores at one price.

**Advanced: $299/month (as of November 2026)**

Described on the pricing page as the plan "for teams scaling multiple storefronts." Everything in Pro, plus a Slack support channel and advanced analytics. The limits stay the same as Pro (5 stores, 10 seats, and 5 custom domains), and a dedicated success manager is not included (as of November 2026).

**Plus: Contact sales (as of November 2026)**

Built for high-volume commerce. Everything in Advanced, plus unlimited stores and seats, unlimited monthly AI credits, dedicated AI capacity, and a dedicated success manager (as of November 2026). Pricing is set by talking to the Amboras team.

## Free to Start, Plus a 30-Day Launch Guarantee on Paid Plans

The direct answer to "Can I try Amboras for free?" is yes, as of November 2026: you can build a store on the free tier with no credit card, but you need a paid plan to publish on a custom domain or use paid features.

In addition, the Basic, Pro, and Advanced plans come with a **30-day launch guarantee** (as of November 2026): Amboras commits to getting your store live within 24 hours, and if no sales occur in the first 30 days, the full subscription fee is refunded. This isn't a standard free trial — it's a performance-backed guarantee, which reflects confidence in the AI's ability to drive early results.

Plan changes are available at any time — upgrades or downgrades — with prorated billing applied immediately and no data migration required. That flexibility makes it practical to start at one tier and adjust once you have real usage data.

## Transaction Fees

Based on a thorough review of Amboras' official Pricing page, there are no additional platform-level transaction fees beyond the base plan cost. Payments process through Stripe, so standard Stripe processing rates apply — typically 2.9% + $0.30 per transaction for domestic cards, with additional rates for international cards.

This is a meaningful structural difference from Shopify. Shopify charges an additional transaction fee when merchants use any payment processor other than Shopify Payments: 2% on Basic, 1% on Grow, and 0.6% on Advanced, according to its pricing page as of November 2026. Amboras' fee structure is simply: **plan fee + Stripe processing** — no additional platform percentage on transactions.

Note: Stripe's exact rates vary by country and contract, so confirm your specific terms with Stripe before committing.

## Migration from Shopify Is Free

For merchants on Shopify considering a switch, Amboras' official FAQ states that all paid plans include free migration of products, customers, orders, and URL redirects from Shopify, with most migrations completing within 48 hours. SEO redirect handling is included, which matters for merchants who have built organic traffic over time.

The practical implication: migration risk is lower than it might appear. The recommended starting point is to calculate your current total monthly cost — plan fee plus all active app subscriptions — and compare it against the equivalent Amboras plan. The delta is often larger than expected.

## Amboras Pricing for Selling Internationally

If you plan to sell internationally, the Pro plan ($105/month, billed monthly) is the practical starting point on Amboras, because it is the lowest tier that includes multi-currency and automatic tax. As of November 2026, the Amboras pricing page shows that "Multi-currency and automatic tax" is not included in the Basic plan ($49/month).

The main costs of selling internationally on Amboras are (as of November 2026):

1. **Monthly fee**: Pro is $105/month and Advanced is $299/month on monthly billing, with 20% off on annual billing. Because billing is in USD, merchants who earn in other currencies will see their effective cost move with exchange rates.
2. **Payment processing**: The Amboras FAQ says processing fees are passed through at cost, typically 2.9% + 30¢ for US cards and lower in the EU, and that Amboras adds no separate transaction fee on top.
3. **Shipping and duties**: According to the Amboras FAQ, shipping rates can be set by zone, weight, or order value, and you can print labels at discounted rates from integrated carriers. Duties can either be collected at checkout (DDP) or left to the carrier (DAP).

When comparing costs, include fees charged on sales, not just the monthly price. ## FAQ

**Q1. Is there a free trial? Any way to test Amboras at no cost?**

Yes. As of November 2026, you can build and test a store on the free tier with no credit card. Publishing on a custom domain and paid features require a paid plan, and the Basic, Pro, and Advanced plans come with the 30-day launch guarantee: if the store generates zero sales in the first 30 days, the full subscription is refunded. In practice, this functions as a risk-free evaluation period — you pay to start, but you're protected if the platform doesn't deliver.

**Q2. How much does annual billing actually save?**

Annual billing reduces the price by 20%. As of November 2026, Basic costs $470.40/year (saving $117.60 versus monthly billing) and Pro costs $1,008/year (saving $252). For businesses with stable revenue projections, switching to annual billing is worth evaluating.

**Q3. Which plan is best for cross-border e-commerce?**

As of November 2026, multi-currency and automatic tax become available from the Pro plan. For serious cross-border operations, Pro is the recommended minimum. If you plan to run separate storefronts for different languages or regions, Pro's five-stores-under-one-subscription model makes that structure efficient and cost-effective.

**Q4. How much are payment fees when overseas customers pay by card?**

A. The Amboras FAQ says processing fees are passed through at cost, typically 2.9% + 30¢ for US cards and lower in the EU (as of November 2026). Amboras says it adds no separate transaction fee on top, but the FAQ doesn't specify the terms for internationally issued cards or currency conversion, so ask Amboras to confirm before you sign up.

## Conclusion

Amboras pricing can be summarized in one principle: **the monthly fee includes the AI**. On traditional platforms like Shopify, email automation, A/B testing, product image enhancement, and multilingual support each require separate app subscriptions — costs that accumulate faster than most merchants anticipate. Amboras consolidates that stack into a single monthly fee, which makes its all-in cost competitive — particularly for multi-store or cross-border operations.

The qualification remains: Shopify's platform maturity and ecosystem depth are unmatched. For merchants with complex technical requirements or a need for highly specific integrations, Shopify remains the more proven choice. For teams who want AI to handle the operational layer — and want a clear, unconvoluted cost structure — Amboras is worth a serious look.
