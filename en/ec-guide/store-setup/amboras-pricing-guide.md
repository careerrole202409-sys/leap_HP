## Amboras Pricing at a Glance

Amboras is an AI-native e-commerce platform selected for Y Combinator's Spring 2026 batch. It handles store design generation, A/B testing, and automated email campaigns through AI — covering nearly the entire operational stack without external app integrations. That combination has generated significant interest among e-commerce operators.

This article covers the pricing structure in full: the monthly fees and feature differences across the four plans (Basic, Grow, Advanced, Enterprise), how the 30-day launch guarantee works, transaction fee structure, and a side-by-side cost comparison with Shopify.

The short answer: Amboras has no free plan. The entry point is the Basic plan at $39/month. What sets it apart is that AI automation tools — image enhancement, email automation, A/B testing, and more — are all built in at every tier. When those tools are factored against the cost of equivalent Shopify apps, Amboras' all-in pricing often compares favorably.

## The Four Amboras Plans

Amboras pricing is structured in four tiers, designed to scale with business size. Both monthly and annual billing (approximately 20% off) are available. The prices below reflect monthly billing.

**Basic: $39/month**

Described on the Amboras site as "everything you need to start selling today." Suited to individual merchants and early-stage startups. Includes: AI Store Designer, unlimited AI product image enhancement, email campaigns and flow automation, native product reviews, 150+ payment methods, a high-conversion-optimized checkout, custom domain, and 24/7 chat support. Covers 1 store, 1 user.

**Grow: $105/month**

The plan officially described as most popular, designed for small-to-midsize e-commerce teams scaling a brand. Everything in Basic, plus: up to 5 stores under one subscription, 5 team members, autonomous A/B and A/B/n testing, advanced analytics and cohort reporting, multi-currency, multilingual support and automated tax calculation, post-purchase upsell and cart abandonment recovery, and priority chat support. The standout feature for cross-border merchants: 5 stores at one price.

**Advanced: $399/month**

Built for merchants running a portfolio of stores, or businesses that need a dedicated Customer Success Manager and white-label options. Everything in Grow, plus: up to 20 stores, 20 team members, 20 custom domains, a dedicated CSM, private Slack channel, AI custom fine-tuning trained on your brand voice, and a white-label storefront option.

**Enterprise: Custom pricing**

Everything in Advanced, plus: full sandbox cloning of existing stores for A/B testing, custom autonomous A/B testing, AI agent-optimized analytics, cross-channel custom attribution tracking, unlimited stores, team members, and custom domains, plus a dedicated account manager and onboarding specialist. Pricing is negotiated based on scale.

## No Free Plan — But a 30-Day Launch Guarantee

The direct answer to "Can I try Amboras for free?" is: not with a free plan. There is no permanently free tier.

However, all paid plans come with a **30-day launch guarantee**: Amboras commits to getting your store live within 24 hours, and if no sales occur in the first 30 days, the full subscription fee is refunded. This isn't a standard free trial — it's a performance-backed guarantee, which reflects confidence in the AI's ability to drive early results.

Plan changes are available at any time — upgrades or downgrades — with prorated billing applied immediately and no data migration required. That flexibility makes it practical to start at one tier and adjust once you have real usage data.

## Transaction Fees

Based on a thorough review of Amboras' official Pricing page, there are no additional platform-level transaction fees beyond the base plan cost. Payments process through Stripe, so standard Stripe processing rates apply — typically 2.9% + $0.30 per transaction for domestic cards, with additional rates for international cards.

This is a meaningful structural difference from Shopify. Shopify charges an additional transaction fee when merchants use any payment processor other than Shopify Payments: 2% on Basic, 1% on Grow, 0.5% on Advanced. Amboras' fee structure is simply: **plan fee + Stripe processing** — no additional platform percentage on transactions.

Note: Stripe's exact rates vary by country and contract, so confirm your specific terms with Stripe before committing.

Adding these costs to Shopify Basic frequently produces a total that exceeds Amboras Basic.

For merchants running or planning to run multiple storefronts, Amboras Grow's five-stores-for-one-subscription model creates a structural cost advantage. On Shopify, each additional store requires a separate plan subscription. The per-store economics diverge quickly as store count increases.

## Migration from Shopify Is Free

For merchants on Shopify considering a switch, Amboras' official FAQ states that all paid plans include free migration of products, customers, orders, and URL redirects from Shopify, with most migrations completing within 48 hours. SEO redirect handling is included, which matters for merchants who have built organic traffic over time.

The practical implication: migration risk is lower than it might appear. The recommended starting point is to calculate your current total monthly cost — plan fee plus all active app subscriptions — and compare it against the equivalent Amboras plan. The delta is often larger than expected.

## Merchant Reviews: G2 and Capterra

Amboras is a new platform, having launched publicly in 2025–2026, and review volume on G2 and Capterra remains limited. The credibility signal is primarily the Y Combinator selection. Across available user comments and the testimonials on the Amboras website, three themes recur consistently: the speed of setup (stores launching within minutes), the AI's continuous optimization without manual intervention, and the reduction in dependency on external plugins and agencies.

The honest caveat: Amboras' platform maturity doesn't yet match Shopify's. Merchants with complex customization requirements or deep ERP integrations may encounter limitations. Shopify's app ecosystem — 8,000+ apps — provides a depth of integrations that Amboras is still building toward. This tradeoff belongs in any honest evaluation.

## FAQ

**Q1. Is there a free trial? Any way to test Amboras at no cost?**

No permanently free plan and no time-limited free trial are currently offered. All paid plans come with the 30-day launch guarantee: if the store generates zero sales in the first 30 days, the full subscription is refunded. In practice, this functions as a risk-free evaluation period — you pay to start, but you're protected if the platform doesn't deliver.

**Q2. How much does annual billing actually save?**

Annual billing reduces the monthly rate by approximately 20%. Basic drops from $39/month to around $31/month, saving approximately $96/year. Grow drops from $105/month to around $84/month, saving approximately $252/year. For businesses with stable revenue projections, switching to annual billing is worth evaluating.

**Q3. Which plan is best for cross-border e-commerce?**

Multi-currency, multilingual support, and automated tax calculation become available from the Grow plan. For serious cross-border operations, Grow is the recommended minimum. If you plan to run separate storefronts for different languages or regions, Grow's five-stores-under-one-subscription model makes that structure efficient and cost-effective.

## Conclusion

Amboras pricing can be summarized in one principle: **the monthly fee includes the AI**. On traditional platforms like Shopify, email automation, A/B testing, product image enhancement, and multilingual support each require separate app subscriptions — costs that accumulate faster than most merchants anticipate. Amboras consolidates that stack into a single monthly fee, which makes its all-in cost competitive — particularly for multi-store or cross-border operations.

The qualification remains: Shopify's platform maturity and ecosystem depth are unmatched. For merchants with complex technical requirements or a need for highly specific integrations, Shopify remains the more proven choice. For teams who want AI to handle the operational layer — and want a clear, unconvoluted cost structure — Amboras is worth a serious look. The 30-day launch guarantee provides a meaningful way to test that conclusion with real data.
