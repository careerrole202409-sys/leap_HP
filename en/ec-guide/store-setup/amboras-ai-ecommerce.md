## [Quick Summary] Amboras: The AI-Native Platform That Makes Your Store Run Itself

Amboras is an e-commerce platform founded in 2025 and accepted into Y Combinator (Spring 2026 batch) — one of the world's most selective startup accelerators. Its defining concept sets it apart from Shopify, BASE, and every other traditional platform: AI that automatically builds your store and continuously optimizes it over time.

This guide covers Amboras from the ground up, drawing on official sources:

* What Amboras is and how it came to exist
* Key features: AI Store Builder, AI Shop System, generative A/B testing, and more
* How it differs fundamentally from Shopify, BASE, and other established platforms
* Language support and step-by-step registration guide
* Pricing plans and considerations for international merchants

If you're stretched thin running an e-commerce operation, or if Shopify app fees and agency costs are eating into your margins, this guide is for you.

## Why Amboras Was Built — A Challenge to the "Static Store" Model

Amboras was founded in 2025 by brothers Imad Mokadem and Amin Mokadem. Both studied at ETH Zurich — one in mechanical engineering, the other in computer science — and while still in school, built their own DTC brand selling educational board games, scaling it to over $200,000 in monthly revenue. In doing so, they ran straight into what they came to see as the fundamental limitations of platforms like Shopify.

The core problem, in their framing: modern e-commerce platforms were not built for the AI era. On existing platforms, changing your store design requires a designer. Improving conversion rates (CVR) means hiring a CRO agency. Extending functionality means sorting through 20,000+ third-party apps, each with its own monthly fee. Serious e-commerce operators commonly spend over $500 per month on apps alone — and after all that overhead, the store itself learns nothing from the customers who visit it. It stays static.

The brothers initially tried to address one slice of this problem with EcomCoder, a Shopify development tool that grew to 1,000 users. But they concluded that patching one part of the stack wasn't enough — the entire stack needed to be rebuilt from scratch. Amboras is the result. Y Combinator describes it simply as "AI-Native Shopify." The founders don't position it as an incremental upgrade; they call it "e-commerce version 2.0."

## Amboras' Key Features — A Store That Runs on Prompts

### AI Store Builder: From One Sentence to a Live Store in Under Two Minutes

The most striking capability of Amboras is its AI-powered store creation. Type a single sentence — say, "I want to sell hand-stitched leather boxing gloves" — and the AI executes the following tasks in parallel:

It proposes and generates a brand name (short and memorable). It creates a hero product plus two related products — complete with 150–200-word descriptions, variant configurations, pricing, and publishing. Simultaneously, it sets brand colors, a slogan, and a logo, and configures promotions: bundle discounts, a free shipping threshold, and a welcome coupon. A live preview is available the moment a URL is issued. The entire process takes under two minutes. Amboras frames this as "launching from zero." For merchants with an existing store, free migration is available and completes within 48 hours.

### AI Shop System: Your Entire Admin Panel as an AI Assistant

Every page of the admin panel has a dedicated AI assistant. On the Orders page, the AI specializes in order management. On the Products page, it's ready to write copy and generate images. You can type or speak your instructions — "Send a retargeting email to customers who purchased last Tuesday," "Create three Black Friday bundle options" — and the AI executes them directly. This is not a chatbot that answers questions. It's an agent that takes action. That distinction is the core differentiator.

### Generative A/B Testing: The Store That Optimizes Itself

The feature that earns Amboras the label "agentic ecommerce" is its autonomous A/B testing engine. The AI continuously generates variants of hero sections, copy, layouts, and offer structures based on live traffic data, automatically launches the tests, and applies the winning version with a single click. According to Amboras, beta merchants saw CVR lifts of over 80% from their first live version. The more traffic a store receives, the smarter it gets — a genuinely self-improving system.

### AI Image Enhancement: Studio-Quality Product Photos, Generated Automatically

Upload a product photo and Amboras' AI generates lifestyle and packshot images at studio quality — background removal, brand tone alignment, and resolution enhancement, all within seconds. For merchants with large catalogs, eliminating the need for external photographers or photo studios is a meaningful and ongoing cost reduction.

### Payments and Analytics, Built In from Day One

Stripe and PayPal are connected from day one. Apple Pay, Google Pay, and address autocomplete are standard. Analytics are built on first-party data — no reliance on external pixels, with all data remaining under the merchant's control.

## Amboras vs. Shopify and BASE — "A Stack of Tools" vs. "One Unified Platform"

### The Structural Problem with Existing Platforms

Take Shopify as the example: the platform itself essentially provides web hosting and a checkout system. Everything else — reviews, landing page builders, email marketing, subscription management, popups, bundle configurations — requires separate third-party apps, each with its own monthly fee. With 21,000+ apps in the Shopify App Store, running a serious store commonly adds $500 or more in monthly overhead. BASE and STORES have lower onboarding friction but offer less extensibility than Shopify.

On all of these platforms, AI is an afterthought — a plugin layer sitting on top of a form-based UI built for a different era. Changing your storefront means opening the theme editor, hiring a designer, briefing a developer, and configuring an A/B test. All of that just to adjust one offer for Black Friday.

### Amboras' Vision: AI as the Interface Itself

Amboras approaches this from the opposite direction. AI is not a tool you add on — it is the admin panel itself. As the product description puts it: rather than "adding an AI app on top of a platform," the design allows "AI to gain new capabilities." The platform grows with the AI rather than constraining it.

## Language Support and Getting Started with Amboras

### Does Amboras Support Multiple Languages?

According to the official FAQ, Amboras supports multilingual operation across the entire admin panel and AI assistant. AI-generated content — product descriptions, SEO metadata, email templates — can be produced in multiple languages. The storefront automatically switches currencies based on the buyer's location, making Amboras suitable for domestic storefronts, cross-border stores, and multilingual international setups.

That said, as of June 2026, Amboras is roughly one year old as a company. Its documentation and community resources are primarily in English, and a dedicated non-English support community is not yet established. Comfort with English-language interfaces and documentation will be an asset for non-English merchants at this stage.

### How to Sign Up

1. Go to amboras.com and click "Start for free"
2. Create an account with your email and password (no credit card required)
3. Enter a one-sentence description of what you want to sell
4. The AI generates your brand name, products, design, and promotions in parallel (~2 minutes)
5. Review the live preview, connect a custom domain, and publish

Migration from existing Shopify, WooCommerce, or BigCommerce stores is available free of charge with a paid plan. Products, customers, order history, and 301 redirects all carry over, with SEO impact minimized.

## Real-World Context: What Amboras Can Do for Your Business

Amboras is a young company, so large-scale public case studies are limited — but the founders' own track record is the most credible reference point available. Imad and Amin Mokadem scaled an educational board game brand on Shopify to over $200,000 in monthly revenue. The problems they encountered firsthand — paying ongoing CRO agency retainers to push CVR above 2%, spending two weeks with designers and developers rebuilding pages for a single sales event — are precisely what Amboras was built to solve.

The Amboras website's "Examples" gallery showcases demo stores built on the platform: "L'Duchen Maison" (luxury watches), "Harvest & Root" (outdoor goods), and "Howl & Honey" (lifestyle products). The design quality is striking for AI-generated work.

## FAQ

### Q1. Do I need programming or design skills to use Amboras?

A. No. Amboras is built on the premise that no code, no theme editor, and no agency should be required. Store creation, editing, and optimization are all handled through text or voice instructions to the AI assistant. The official FAQ answers "Do I need any technical skills to use Amboras?" with a direct "No." Tasks that would typically require a developer or designer are handled entirely through conversation with the AI.

### Q2. I currently run a Shopify store. Will migrating to Amboras affect my SEO?

A. According to Amboras, the migration process maps URLs, 301 redirects, sitemaps, and structured data one-to-one from Shopify. The company states that "most stores see flat or improved organic traffic after migration." Migration is handled free of charge with a paid plan and typically completes within 48 hours. That said, as a young platform, Amboras' long-term SEO behavior isn't yet fully documented in third-party studies. We recommend monitoring performance carefully and continuing to gather information before committing to a full migration.

### Q3. Does Amboras support cross-border sales and multilingual storefronts?

A. Yes. Amboras includes automatic currency switching based on buyer location, multilingual content generation, and AI translation as standard features. It supports over 125 payment methods, including Alipay, WeChat Pay, SEPA, and iDEAL. The platform is designed for domestic storefronts, cross-border stores, or both simultaneously. The main caveat at this stage is that English remains the primary language for official documentation and support.

## Conclusion: Amboras Is the Next Option for Eliminating E-Commerce Overhead

Amboras is an AI-native platform built to eliminate the resource drain of running an e-commerce business — app subscriptions, CRO agency retainers, developer requests, and design work. Its credentials (founded 2025, Y Combinator-backed) and the founders' firsthand experience scaling a DTC brand to $200K/month make it a credible contender worth watching.

Multilingual support, cross-border functionality, and accessible pricing make it particularly relevant for companies considering international expansion. Starting with the free plan to test whether the platform fits your workflow is the most sensible first step.
