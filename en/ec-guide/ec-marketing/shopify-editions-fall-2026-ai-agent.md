## Quick Overview: Fall '26 Is About Selling Inside AI Agents and Running Your Store with AI

On October 28, 2026, Shopify released its Fall '26 Edition, themed "Exponential," the latest round of its twice-yearly Shopify Editions updates (Shopify Japan followed with a press release on October 29). The headline is agentic commerce: shoppers find products in conversations with AI tools like ChatGPT and Google's AI Mode, and in some cases buy without leaving the chat. Shopify also announced Shopify Connectors, which let merchants manage their store from the AI agents they already use, and a rebuilt Sidekick, its AI assistant, which can now run calculations, write code and search the web.

For small businesses that want to sell internationally, the key point is that each AI channel can only sell to customers in specific countries. According to Shopify's help center (as of November 2026), ChatGPT sells to customers in the United States, while Google AI Mode and Gemini sell to customers in the United States, Canada and Australia. Both allow stores based outside the US. This article walks through the AI features and what they mean for cross-border sellers.

## What Is Shopify Editions Fall '26?

Shopify Editions Fall '26 is Shopify's roundup of product updates for autumn 2026, published on October 28, 2026. Shopify Editions bundles new features from across the platform every six months, and according to Shopify Japan's press release, Fall '26 is the tenth edition.

The official Editions page groups the updates into 11 categories:

1. Agentic
2. Sidekick
3. Online
4. Merchandising
5. Retail
6. Payments
7. Operations
8. Shop
9. Marketing
10. Fulfillment
11. Developers

The fact that the first two categories are both about AI shows where Shopify's focus lies this time. Keep in mind that some features on the Editions page are marked "coming soon" or "early access" (available to a limited set of stores first), so not everything announced is available right away.

## What Is Agentic Commerce?

Agentic commerce is a way of shopping where AI agents help shoppers find, compare and buy products within a conversation. Shopify's own blog describes it as AI agents that "help shoppers discover, compare, and purchase products—all within a conversation."

In traditional ecommerce, shoppers arrive at your store from search engines or ads, then browse and buy on your site. With agentic commerce, a shopper might ask ChatGPT to "find a gift that goes with X," and the AI suggests products from multiple stores and, depending on the channel, completes checkout in the same conversation. For merchants, AI conversations outside your own site become a new storefront.

## Key AI Features in Shopify Fall '26

The AI features in Fall '26 fall into three groups: selling through shoppers' AI agents, running your store with AI, and preparing the store information that AI agents receive.

### Selling inside shoppers' AI agents

The buyer-side features let shoppers find and buy products from Shopify stores within AI conversations.

- **Shop.md (Shop skill):** Point an AI agent such as ChatGPT or Claude to shop.md, and it becomes a personal shopper that can search products, make recommendations and check out with Shop Pay
- **Shop Pay in AI agents:** Shop Pay checkout is available inside Meta's Muse, Claude Duet and Instinct
- **Selling on Google Search and Gemini:** Sell in Google Search, including AI Mode, and the Gemini app with checkout powered by Shopify
- **Universal Checkout:** In the Shop app, shoppers can combine products from multiple merchants in one cart and pay in a single checkout

### Running your store with AI agents

The merchant-side features let you run day-to-day store work by talking to AI.

- **Shopify Connectors:** Manage products, inventory, orders and performance from nine AI agents: ChatGPT, Claude, Claude Duet, Microsoft Copilot, Google Gemini, Grok, Grok Bot, Muse and Perplexity
- **A more capable Sidekick:** Sidekick in the Shopify admin can now run calculations, write code, search the internet and remember what you teach it. Approve a plan once and it can apply multiple changes at a time, such as editing product descriptions across a collection. Merchants approve its changes
- **Canvas:** Shows your entire store on one canvas, where Sidekick can make anything from small tweaks to full redesigns. Available in early access

### Preparing the information AI agents receive: knowledge base and Shopify Catalog

The knowledge base and the Shopify Catalog eligibility view help AI agents describe your store accurately.

The knowledge base is a page where you publish non-product store facts, such as return windows and shipping destinations, for AI agents. According to Shopify's help center, you open it from **Sales channels > Agentic** under **Sources**. Shopify drafts facts from your storefront and admin settings, and only the facts you approve are shared with agents. A knowledge base can hold up to 1,000 facts in total, including generated and additional facts. The older Shopify Knowledge Base app is being replaced by this feature and will be deprecated on January 15, 2027.

Shopify Catalog is how product data reaches AI channels. On the same Agentic page, you can check whether your store is eligible for Shopify Catalog and, if not, why.

## Can You Use Shopify's Agentic Commerce to Sell Internationally?

Yes, but each AI channel has its own rules about which countries' customers you can sell to. Stores based outside the US can sell through ChatGPT and Google AI Mode, but only to customers in a limited set of countries.

### Which countries each AI channel covers (as of November 2026)

We compiled this table from each channel's page in Shopify's help center.

| AI channel | Store location | Eligible customer countries | Where checkout happens |
|---|---|---|---|
| ChatGPT | Can be outside the US | United States | Your online store checkout (in ChatGPT's in-app browser or a new tab) |
| Google AI Mode and Gemini | Any country | United States, Canada, Australia | In the channel when direct checkout is active (rolling out gradually) |
| Microsoft Copilot | Not stated in the help center | United States | In the channel when direct checkout is active |
| Meta | Not stated in the help center | At least one of: Australia, Brazil, Canada, India, Japan, Mexico, New Zealand, Singapore, South Korea, the UK, the US | In the channel when direct checkout is active |

Google AI Mode and Gemini require a Google Merchant Center account. If you turn off direct checkout, or if the customer is not eligible, shoppers can still find your products but are sent to your online store to buy. For ChatGPT and Meta, Shopify's help center states there are no fees beyond standard payment processing fees.

### Requirements across the four AI channels

Shopify's help center lists these main requirements for ChatGPT, Google, Microsoft and Meta:

1. Your products must be eligible for Shopify Catalog
2. You must agree to the Shopify Agentic Storefronts Supplemental Terms of Service
3. Your terms of service, privacy policy, and return and refund policy must be completed
4. Relevant legal disclosures must appear in the first 6,000 characters of product descriptions (stated on the ChatGPT, Google and Microsoft pages)
5. B2B-only products aren't supported (Google, Microsoft and Meta also exclude subscription-only, bundled and customizable products)

The B2B restriction matters if you plan to sell wholesale to overseas buyers. Shopify's help center notes that agentic storefronts support only direct-to-consumer (D2C) sales.

## Other Fall '26 Features Relevant to Cross-Border Sellers

Fall '26 also includes non-agent features that matter for selling internationally, though some come with regional or plan restrictions.

- **Right-to-left languages:** Launch your online store in Arabic, Hebrew or Urdu. With the Translate & Adapt app, every edit to your store stays current across translated versions
- **More languages:** Shopify now supports 13 additional languages, including Basque, Bengali and Ukrainian
- **New local payment methods:** Seven methods added to Shopify Payments: Alipay, WeChat Pay, MB WAY, OXXO, Satispay, Scalapay and GrabPay. Check your admin to confirm what's available for your store
- **Shopify Managed Markets:** Offer international pricing with guaranteed duties and taxes and flat shipping rates at checkout. Note that Shopify's help center says Managed Markets is available to merchants based in the continental US and to certain stores in Canada and the UK
- **Shop Campaigns:** Now available in more than 35 new markets, including the UK, Australia and the EU
- **Shopify Smart Pricing:** Market-based pricing guidance for newly active products

## How Small Businesses Can Prepare Now

The most useful thing you can do now is make sure the store information AI reads is accurate and available in your customers' languages. These steps help whether or not you use Shopify.

1. **Complete your policy pages:** Terms of service, privacy policy, and return and refund policies are a requirement on all four of Shopify's AI channels
2. **Write specific product descriptions:** Include materials, sizes, how to use the product and which countries you ship to. AI recommends products based on public information, so thin descriptions are less likely to surface
3. **Spell out shipping destinations and return terms:** As the knowledge base examples show, AI agents pass details like return windows and shipping destinations on to shoppers
4. **Start with eligible countries:** ChatGPT covers the US, and Google AI Mode covers the US, Canada and Australia. Starting with whichever eligible market fits your products best is the realistic first step
5. **Publish content in local languages:** Overseas shoppers ask AI questions in their own language, so product information and blog posts in English and other local languages help

## FAQ

**Q. When was Shopify Editions Fall '26 released?**

A. It was released on October 28, 2026. Shopify Japan published its press release on October 29. Shopify Editions bundles new features every six months, and Fall '26 is the tenth edition.

**Q. What is agentic commerce?**

A. Agentic commerce is a way of shopping where AI agents help shoppers find, compare and buy products within a conversation. On Shopify, products can appear in ChatGPT, Google AI Mode and Gemini, Microsoft Copilot and Meta, and some channels let shoppers buy without leaving the chat.

**Q. Can a Shopify store based outside the US sell through Google AI Mode?**

A. Yes. Shopify's help center says your store can be based in any country. However, eligible customers are in the United States, Canada and Australia, and direct checkout is rolling out gradually (as of November 2026). You also need a Google Merchant Center account, and your products must be eligible for Shopify Catalog.

**Q. Can I manage inventory and orders with Shopify's AI agents?**

A. Yes. Shopify Connectors, announced in Fall '26, let you manage products, inventory, orders and performance from nine AI agents, including ChatGPT and Claude. Inside the Shopify admin, you can also use Sidekick.

## Summary and Key Takeaway

Shopify Editions Fall '26 prepares merchants for a world where products are discovered and bought inside AI conversations. On the buyer side, you can sell through ChatGPT, Google AI Mode, Meta and more. On the merchant side, Shopify Connectors and Sidekick let you run your store with AI. The knowledge base and Shopify Catalog connect the two.

From a cross-border perspective, the real takeaway is that AI channels open up country by country. Stores outside the US can take part, but eligible customers are limited to places like the US, Canada and Australia, and B2B-only products are excluded. And on every channel, AI can only recommend what your public product and store information says. Getting ready to be picked by AI starts not with new settings, but with clearly writing your product, shipping and returns information in the languages of the markets you want to reach.
