## Quick Overview: Understanding the Acquisition Mechanism Matters More Than Browsing a Shop Directory

Most people searching a "STORES shop directory" don't just want to browse other shops' designs — they want to know how those shops actually drive traffic. Rather than listing designs, this guide analyzes what STORES's own survey data shows about SNS usage, the official push behind Instagram-driven acquisition, and real examples of distinctive independent shops on the platform, to work out how to design your own acquisition path.

## What the Official Survey Shows About "Which SNS Actually Drives Traffic"

According to a survey by Bracket Inc., the company behind STORES, the SNS platform most frequently cited as effective for promoting and driving traffic to an online shop is Instagram, at 41.4%. That a visual, photo-and-video-first platform like Instagram comes out on top makes sense as a way to communicate a product's appeal — and it's a useful, concrete data point if you're unsure which SNS platform to prioritize first.

## STORES's Official Push Behind Instagram Acquisition

STORES doesn't just publish survey findings that "Instagram is effective" — it also actively shares guidance on how to actually use it. The platform has co-hosted a webinar focused on Instagram strategy, titled "The Secret to Growing E-Commerce Sales Through Instagram — Strategy and Real Examples." It's also partnered with FinT, an SNS consulting firm, to offer STORES users exclusive pricing on SNS consulting and managed social media operation. If you don't have the time to build up Instagram expertise entirely on your own, these official support options are worth considering.

## Real Examples of Distinctive Independent Shops

STORES runs a program called "STORES ASSIST PROGRAM," designed to support individuals and small teams running their own shops. Among the finalists named for this program are real, independently run shops like YUMI YOSHIMOTO ONLINE STORE, Enpitsudo, and YAMANOVA — shops run by individuals or small teams rather than large companies, each developing a distinctive identity on STORES. When you're looking for design or strategy inspiration, it's worth looking beyond big-brand case studies to shops at this smaller scale, which can offer more grounded, practical ideas.

## Designing Your Own Acquisition Path, Based on the Evidence

Pulling this together, a few things stand out for designing your acquisition path on STORES. First, building around Instagram is a realistic first choice, backed up by actual survey data. Consistently posting photos and videos that convey your product's appeal, and driving traffic from those posts to your shop page, is the baseline. Second, if building up SNS expertise entirely in-house is difficult, STORES's own webinars and its SNS consulting partnership are worth leaning on. Third, when looking for design or acquisition inspiration, it's worth looking at distinctive shops run by individuals or small teams, not just large-scale case studies.

## FAQ

**Q. Is Instagram the only SNS platform that works for acquisition on STORES?**

A. STORES's own survey shows Instagram as the most-cited platform, but that doesn't mean other platforms have no effect. Depending on your product category and target customers, other SNS platforms can work well too. A practical approach is starting with Instagram, given the survey data, then testing other channels based on your own customer base.

**Q. Can I outsource SNS operation?**

A. Yes — STORES has partnered with SNS consulting firm FinT to offer STORES users exclusive pricing on consulting and managed social media operation. If you can't dedicate in-house resources to this, it's worth considering.

**Q. Can an individual or small team actually stand out on STORES?**

A. As the finalists in the "STORES ASSIST PROGRAM" show, individuals and small teams without major capital do run distinctive, successful shops on STORES. What matters more than scale is how well you communicate your product's and brand's identity.

## Summary and Key Takeaway

Analyze what actually drives acquisition for successful STORES shops, and what surfaces isn't some hidden trick — it's the fairly ordinary combination of steady Instagram-driven content and making use of the official support STORES provides. Polishing a shop's design is only the starting point; what actually matters is building a mechanism that keeps bringing people back to that shop, consistently.
