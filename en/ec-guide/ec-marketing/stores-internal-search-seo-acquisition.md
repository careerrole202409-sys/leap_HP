## Quick Overview: It's Not That You Can't Be Found "On STORES" — There's No Marketplace Search to Begin With

Most people searching "STORES search" are frustrated that their shop isn't getting found, and traffic has stalled. The first thing worth understanding: STORES isn't a marketplace like Amazon or Rakuten, where you can search across every seller's products in one place — each shop built on STORES runs as its own independent site. In other words, most of the frustration around "my shop can't be found on STORES" actually comes from there being no cross-shop search mechanism to begin with. With that in mind, this guide covers how to write product names and descriptions for Google search, Google Merchant Center integration, and a hashtag strategy for driving search traffic from SNS.

## There's No "Marketplace-Wide Search" on STORES

Independent store builders like BASE and STORES work fundamentally differently from marketplace platforms like Rakuten or Amazon. On a marketplace, typing a product name into the marketplace's own search box pulls up products from many different sellers at once. A shop built on STORES, by contrast, is its own independent site with its own URL — there's no mechanism designed to search across all STORES shops at once. The starting point for thinking about acquisition isn't "how do I get found inside STORES" — it's "how do people actually reach my shop, through Google or SNS." We weren't able to fully confirm, from official sources at the time of writing, whether there's a feature for adding a product search box inside an individual shop page, or how to configure one. It's worth checking STORES's own help center directly for that specific setting.

## Writing Product Names and Descriptions for Google Search

With no marketplace-wide search, getting found through an external search engine like Google becomes the key lever. The basic idea is working the words someone actually searching would use into your product name and description, naturally. For example, going beyond just a brand name to include specific, searchable details — color, size, intended use — helps search engines correctly understand what a product page is about. A product description also shouldn't just list specs; writing in a way that conveys whose problem the product solves and how helps convert the traffic that search brings in, not just attract it.

## Boosting Your Presence in Search Results With Google Merchant Center

According to STORES's official site, it offers integration with Google Merchant Center, which, once connected, gets your product images showing up in Google search results. Beyond text-based optimization of product names and descriptions, taking advantage of this kind of built-in integration strengthens how your products actually appear in search results. Once you've put the work into your product pages, it's worth not skipping this integration setup.

## A Hashtag Strategy for Driving Search Traffic From SNS

Alongside Google search, SNS-driven search traffic is another important channel. As covered in a separate guide, STORES's own survey found Instagram to be the most frequently cited SNS platform for driving acquisition. A key piece of making the most of Instagram is how you use hashtags. Beyond generic hashtags that just describe a product category, combining your brand name with specific, searchable keywords a prospective buyer would actually use can boost traffic from in-app search and hashtag browsing. Rather than reusing the same hashtags on every post, revisiting which words are actually searched for, product by product, is the kind of unglamorous but effective work that compounds over time.

The real issue behind "my products aren't being found" usually isn't a missing feature — it's not having deliberately built out the paths that lead people from external search engines and SNS to your shop. ## FAQ

**Q. Does STORES have a search box within shops?**

A. We weren't able to fully confirm, from official sources at the time of writing, whether a product search box feature exists within individual shop pages, or how to set one up. Given that there's no marketplace-wide search across STORES as a whole, it's worth prioritizing traffic from Google search and SNS instead.

**Q. Will stuffing my product name with keywords get it ranked higher in search results?**

A. Forcing in irrelevant keywords isn't a good idea. What matters is naturally including the words a prospective buyer would actually search for in your product name and description. Add specific details without sacrificing readability.

**Q. Do more hashtags mean better results?**

A. Relevance to your product and brand matters more than sheer quantity. Overly generic hashtags risk getting buried among countless other posts, so combining your brand name with specific keywords tends to work better.

## Summary and Key Takeaway

The first realization worth having about "my products aren't being found on STORES" is that STORES itself has no marketplace-wide search to begin with. Once that's clear, the right moves naturally focus on one question: how do people actually find you through Google search and SNS. Refining your product names and descriptions, setting up Google Merchant Center integration, and running a deliberate hashtag strategy on SNS aren't flashy tricks, but building them up consistently is what actually raises the odds of being found.
