## Quick Overview: Each Platform's Acquisition Strength Sits in a Different Place

Most people comparing BASE, STORES, and Color Me Shop (a Japanese store-builder platform) want to know more than just the fee differences — they want to know which one actually fits their acquisition strategy. The short answer: all three put their acquisition strength in a different place. BASE leans on its in-app buyer community, STORES leans on external SNS integration, and Color Me Shop leans on SEO and content on your own domain. This guide covers the fee and commission differences between the three, each platform's acquisition approach, where things stand on email marketing, and how to choose based on your own acquisition strategy.

## Fee and Commission Differences

As a starting point, the three platforms' pricing structures are built on fundamentally different models.

BASE's official pricing page shows a Standard plan with no setup fee or monthly fee, charging a 3.6% payment processing fee plus ¥40 per order, plus a 3% service fee — roughly 6.6% of sales plus ¥40 per order. It's a purely performance-based model: costs only arise when you actually sell something.

STORES offers two tiers: a Free plan (¥0/month, processing fee from 2.48%) and a Standard plan (¥3,300/month under an annual contract, processing fee from 1.98%). It's a hybrid model — paying a monthly fee lowers your processing fee.

Color Me Shop runs on fixed monthly plans starting with its Regular plan (from ¥4,950/month, processing fee from 3.4%), and its official site explicitly states "0 yen sales commission." In other words, beyond the card processing fee, no additional percentage-based commission is charged as sales grow — a fixed monthly cost model.

In short: BASE is built for starting small and paying only when you sell; STORES balances a monthly fee against a lower processing rate; Color Me Shop is designed so your fee burden doesn't keep climbing as monthly sales grow.

## BASE's Acquisition Strength: Its In-App Buyer Community

BASE's acquisition hub is its buyer-facing app, "PAY ID." Buyers who sign up for PAY ID can receive new arrivals and promotions in-app from the shops they follow, and shops can expand their visibility inside the app by building up followers. BASE also offers a free "Email Newsletter App" for sending your own newsletters, and as of April 30, 2026, that app added a feature where AI suggests subject lines, headlines, and body copy. BASE's acquisition strength lies in how effectively you can tap into its in-platform user base.

## STORES's Acquisition Strength: External SNS Integration

STORES builds its acquisition features around integration with external SNS and content. Its flagship feature is "Instagram Shopping Integration," which lets you tag products from your shop directly in Instagram posts, driving traffic straight to your product pages. It also offers a "NEWS" feature for publishing blog-style articles related to your products directly on your shop, which serves as a foundation for organic search traffic. STORES also positions itself less as a pure online-store builder and more as a broader "store operations platform" covering cashless payments, POS registers, and reservation systems for physical stores — making it a natural fit for businesses that want to manage online and offline acquisition together.

## Color Me Shop's Acquisition Strength: SEO on Your Own Domain

Where Color Me Shop stands apart from the other two is SEO. According to its official site, Color Me Shop lets you run WordPress on your shop's own domain for free. Many competing services charge extra for installing a blog on the same domain, so offering this for free is a real advantage for businesses that want to build long-term SEO traffic through blog content without splitting their domain authority. Color Me Shop also includes an email newsletter feature as a standard part of the platform, bundling everything from product page creation to analytics into a single service.

## Choosing Based on Your Own Acquisition Strategy

Comparing the three: BASE fits well if you want to grow a new fan base through visibility inside its app's user community. STORES fits businesses that already have an Instagram following, or that want to connect online acquisition with a physical store. Color Me Shop tends to pay off most for businesses prioritizing long-term search traffic built through accumulated blog content.

Whichever you choose, keep in mind BASE's own guidance, which states plainly that "unlike marketplace-style services, you need to attract customers yourself." None of these platforms drive traffic to your shop automatically. Each platform's acquisition features are a foundation that supports your own acquisition work — and no matter which feature you build around, filling out product pages and publishing content consistently remains essential.

Deciding which platform, and which feature, to build your acquisition strategy around is a decision that shapes your business's growth far more than the act of building the store itself. ## FAQ

**Q. Which of the three has the lowest fees?**

A. It depends on your monthly sales volume. At smaller scale, BASE's performance-based model keeps your burden low. As monthly sales grow, STORES's Standard plan or Color Me Shop's fixed monthly fee tend to lower your fee burden as a percentage of sales. It's worth running the comparison against your own store's actual monthly sales.

**Q. If I want to prioritize SEO, should I just pick Color Me Shop?**

A. Being able to run WordPress for free on your own domain is a clear advantage for Color Me Shop. But SEO results come from consistent content work over time, not from the tool alone. Whichever platform you choose, you'll need to commit to building out blog content and product pages.

**Q. Which is more effective — BASE's PAY ID app or STORES's Instagram integration?**

A. Neither is objectively better; it comes down to which fits the strength you already have. STORES makes more sense if you want to leverage an existing Instagram following, while BASE makes more sense if you're looking to build new visibility among app users from scratch.

## Summary and Key Takeaway

There's no simple answer to "which of BASE, STORES, and Color Me Shop is strongest for acquisition." The three platforms put their acquisition strength in entirely different places: an in-platform user base (BASE), external SNS integration (STORES), and SEO on your own domain (Color Me Shop). Their fee structures also differ fundamentally — performance-based, hybrid, and fixed monthly cost — and which is the better deal depends on your monthly sales volume. Choosing a platform isn't the finish line of building a store; it's the starting point for deciding which acquisition channel you'll spend the coming months building your business around.
