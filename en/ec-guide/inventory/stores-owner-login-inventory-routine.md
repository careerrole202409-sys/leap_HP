## Quick Overview: What You Do After Logging In Matters More Than Logging In Itself

Most people searching "STORES owner login" aren't just after the login steps — they want to know what to actually do once they're logged in, and how to make daily inventory management more efficient. This guide covers the basics of logging in to STORES's shop admin, the order confirmation and stock reconciliation work you should do after logging in, how inventory actually syncs with STORES Register, and what to do if you can't log in.

## The Basics of Logging In to the STORES Admin

As a shop owner on STORES, you access your admin dashboard starting from the official site (stores.jp), signing in with the email and password you registered when you set up your shop. The admin dashboard and the customer-facing shop page are separate screens — it's worth being careful not to confuse the page where customers browse and buy with the page where you, as the owner, manage products and orders. The exact login screen and steps can change over time, so it's worth checking STORES's own help center for the current procedure.

## Order Confirmation and Stock Reconciliation After You Log In

Once you're in the admin dashboard, confirming new orders is an essential part of daily operations. When an order comes in, it shows up in your order list, so the first step is checking for new orders and identifying which ones need to be shipped. Once you've shipped an order, you mark it as shipped in the dashboard and reconcile the stock count to make sure it's been decremented correctly — this reconciliation step matters. Skip it, and the gap between your actual stock and what the dashboard shows can grow, risking a sold-out item getting sold again by mistake.

## How Inventory Actually Syncs With STORES Register

For businesses running a physical location, inventory syncing with "STORES Register," the POS system, matters a great deal. A 2022 survey on STORES Register adoption found that among shops using both the register and the online store together, 76.4% actually use the inventory-linking feature between their physical and online channels. The same survey found apparel and fashion was the top adopting industry at 24.4%, with retail overall making up roughly 60% of users. For a business running both a physical shop and an online store, using this inventory-linking feature can significantly cut down on the burden of manually tracking two separate stock counts for online and offline reconciliation.

## What to Do If You Can't Log In

The most common causes of login trouble are a mistyped password or forgetting the email address you registered. As with most web services, the login screen typically includes a way to reset your password. Try resetting with the email address you think you used, and if that doesn't resolve it, reach out to STORES's official support. Sometimes the issue comes down to cached browser data or old login information — trying a different browser or an incognito/private window is one thing worth trying.

## FAQ

**Q. Is the STORES admin dashboard the same URL as the customer-facing shop page?**

A. No, they're separate screens. The admin dashboard, where you register products and manage orders as the owner, is different from the page where customers browse and purchase. Make sure you're accessing the owner-side dashboard when you log in.

**Q. Can I manage inventory without using STORES Register?**

A. Yes. Even with just an online store, you can manage stock counts manually from the admin dashboard. The integration with STORES Register is especially useful for businesses that want to unify inventory tracking across a physical location and an online store.

**Q. Where should I turn if I can't log in?**

A. If resetting your password doesn't resolve it, it's worth contacting STORES's official support. Having your registered email address and shop details on hand makes the inquiry go more smoothly.
