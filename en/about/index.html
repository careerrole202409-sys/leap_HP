Company Profile

At Leap, we develop our organizational culture and the company itself from scratch, just like our products. We strive to create a society where every business can leap onto the global stage.

Mission

Empowering every business to leap globally

Leap is driven by the mission to "empower every business to leap globally." Our purpose is not to pursue superficial growth (Growth) through numbers alone, but to support a true "Leap"—delivering essential value across borders and into new markets.

Our Culture

We uphold three core values to deliver the highest results to our clients at the fastest speed.

Responsibility

Every individual takes full ownership of the quality of output provided to our clients, seeing every professional task through to completion.

Focus

To elevate our output, we sharpen our resources and concentrate only on what matters most.

Speed

Through intense focus, we act faster than anyone else, delivering results at a pace that exceeds market changes.

Our History

Leap was founded in July 2024 by cousins Hara and Tsuruoka, who both sought to contribute to society through Digital Transformation (DX) since their time at the University of Tokyo.

Drawing from his experience in macroeconomic policy at the Cabinet Office, Hara became convinced that the global expansion of Japanese companies is the key to revitalizing the national economy. Meanwhile, at Hitachi, Tsuruoka was engaged in Generative AI research and development, witnessing how AI dramatically lowers international barriers like language and business customs.

"Within the next decade, global operations will become the standard for companies worldwide."

With this conviction, the two established Leap in Yokohama to support business challenges through technology and strategy.
